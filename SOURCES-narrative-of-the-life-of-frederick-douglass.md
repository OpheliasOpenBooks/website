# Narrative of the Life of Frederick Douglass — 1845 edition and provenance

- **Work:** Frederick Douglass, *Narrative of the Life of Frederick Douglass, an American Slave. Written by Himself.* Boston: Anti-Slavery Office, No. 25 Cornhill, 1845. Original English autobiography; no translator.
- **Rights:** The 1845 work is public domain in the United States. [Project Gutenberg #23](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/23) hosts a public-domain transcription.
- **Text:** `public/books/narrative-of-the-life-of-frederick-douglass-1845.txt`. Locally mirrored from [davidmcclure/hilt-2016/texts/douglass.txt](https://github.com/davidmcclure/hilt-2016/blob/master/texts/douglass.txt), itself a Project Gutenberg #23 copy identifying the 2013 revision. Removed the modern Gutenberg distribution header, its trailing license boilerplate, and a later biographical insertion headed “FREDERICK DOUGLASS.” (beginning “Frederick Douglass was born in slavery as Frederick Augustus Washington Bailey…”), which was not part of Douglass's original text. Removed the biography's related entry from the modern contents list. Only the poem heading indentation was normalized for navigation. Original prose and historical prefatory material were otherwise retained.
- **Full historical contents:** Title/imprint/contents; **William Lloyd Garrison's preface**; **Wendell Phillips's letter**; **eleven chapters** in order; **Douglass's appendix** and **“A Parody”**, including the final signature/date and “THE END.” Approximately 40,456 whitespace-separated words. Reader has 16 navigable sections (title/contents plus 15 text sections). Poetry line breaks are retained.
