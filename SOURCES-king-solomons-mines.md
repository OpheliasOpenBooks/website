- The novel and its 1907 authorial prefatory notes are **public domain in the United States**.
- Text transcription: [Project Gutenberg eBook #2166](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2166), historical source [GITenberg/King-Solomon-s-Mines_2166/2166.txt](https://github.com/GITenberg/King-Solomon-s-Mines_2166/blob/master/2166.txt). ## Cover documentation
- The 1885 Cassell first edition was issued in **red cloth with black lettering and pictorial decoration**, as documented in [Christie's first-edition description](https://www.christies.com/lot/lot-4486304/) and [Bonhams' first-issue copy](https://www.bonhams.com/auction/20751/lot/161/haggard-h-rider-king-solomons-mines-first-edition-first-issue-1885/).

It uses documented red cloth, understated black printing, and shield/spear ornament. The actual binding's typographic layout and decoration may differ.
