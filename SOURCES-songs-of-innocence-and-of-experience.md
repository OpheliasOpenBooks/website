# Songs of Innocence and of Experience — William Blake

**Rights.** William Blake (1757–1827), public domain in the United States. *Songs of Innocence* appeared in 1789 and the combined *Songs of Innocence and of Experience* in 1794.

**Exact text edition.** Locally hosted poems from the public-domain **1901 R. Brimley Johnson** edition (London/Guildford), transcribed by **David Price** for [Project Gutenberg eBook #1934](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1934), via the UTF-8 mirror [GITenberg](https://github.com/GITenberg/Songs-of-Innocence-and-Songs-of-Experience_1934). This is a **1901 edition**, not a 1794 facsimile. Contents contain **19 individual Innocence poems and 28 Experience poems (47 entries in total)**. Original text, stanzas, and line breaks are preserved. Printed table-of-contents page numbers and Gutenberg legal boilerplate are excluded. The transcription’s contents table incorrectly lists “The Little Boy Pound” but the actual poem header properly reads “THE LITTLE BOY FOUND”; reader follows the actual heading. The 1901 edition calls the famous poem “THE TIGER”; Blake’s illuminated copies may use different spelling, sequence, and selection. Headings are rendered in title case for navigation, with duplicate titles identified by the book part.

**Original historic title-page art.** Original illuminated combined title page printed/hand-coloured by Blake c. **1825**, *copy Y*, [Metropolitan Museum of Art collection](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/347832).
