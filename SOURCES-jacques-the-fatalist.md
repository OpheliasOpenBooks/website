# Jacques the Fatalist / James the Fatalist and His Master — Denis Diderot

**Literary work:** *Jacques le fataliste et son maître*, first published in French in Paris by Buisson (1796). Written in the eighteenth century and circulated earlier. The complete novel is public domain in the United States.

## Published English narrated edition

**Historic title:** *James the Fatalist and His Master: Translated from the French of Diderot*, anonymously translated, printed for G. G. and J. Robinson, Paternoster Row, London (1797), three volumes. The eighteenth-century anonymous English edition is historically corroborated in the King's College London Radical Translations database, record 3826, which lists translation status as **Integral**.

**Historical source and copies:** <https://archive.org/details/james-the-fatalist-and-his-master-translated-from-the-french-of-diderot-1797>. All **three separate physical volumes** are preserved as scans and downloadable OCR. Local source backups and source-audit checksums are recorded in `sources/james-the-fatalist-1797/`. The OCR transcriptions are also committed in GitHub to support iterative manual editorial QA.

**Hosted reader:** `jacques-the-fatalist.html` (English read & listen), with 26 English reading parts under `public/books/james-the-fatalist-1797-part-1.json` through `...-26.json` and `public/books/james-the-fatalist-1797-manifest.json`. Part 1–9 cover Volume I, Part 10–18 Volume II, and Part 19–26 Volume III; together the text comprises approximately 100,350 words after routine mechanical OCR layout normalization. Includes the long first-volume dedication "To the Memory of Diderot" and the original volume endings. The reading part boundaries are created for the website, not Diderot's invented chapters. Narration uses the site's shared `public/js/ophelia-narration.js` and device/browser speech, not pre-recorded audio. The English reader has its own localStorage bookmark and links to the French original.

**Important quality limitation:** These texts are **archival optical-character-recognition (OCR) transcriptions, not fully proofread editions**. Original eighteenth-century fonts and aged scans introduce wrong letters, stray headers, broken words, spurious punctuation and other errors. We preserved source volume coverage and implemented limited mechanical reflow: page headings may be omitted, end-of-volume markers made legible, first-paragraph OCR glitches corrected where unambiguous, and print line-break hyphenations joined. No modern copyrighted translation has been used. **Narration and on-screen text will contain OCR mistakes until human page-by-page correction against the scans is finished.** Do not claim the English text is editorially verified or completely accurate.

## Published French original (read only)

`jacques-le-fataliste-original-francais.html` hosts 23 locally checked original-language parts `public/books/jacques-le-fataliste-original-francais-part-1.json` through `...-23.json`, about 89,300 words. Source: [Project Gutenberg #39976](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/39976), the Jules Assézat nineteenth-century public-domain edited transcription, literary text (source lines 352–14143), excluding his later editorial "Notice préliminaire". Manuscript source comparison passed ignoring whitespace. No French narration, adhering to original-language companion policy. This is not a first-edition photographic facsimile.
