Carry the Library With You

Ophelia’s Open Book, now an app

Our illustrated classics, reading room, narration, and bookmarks—through a convenient icon on your home screen.

Follow the instructions for your device below.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Samsung Internet.
  2. Use Install Ophelia’s App when the button appears, or open the browser menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon.
  3. Select Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Computer

  1. Open the site in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Choose Install from the address bar or browser menu, if offered.
  3. Open the new Ophelia’s icon from your apps or desktop.

This is an installable web app, not yet a separate listing in the Apple App Store or Google Play. Most books and narration still require internet access. Bookmarks and reading position remain subject to the browser’s existing storage, so progress may not automatically transfer between browsers or devices.
