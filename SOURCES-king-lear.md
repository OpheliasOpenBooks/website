# King Lear — Shakespeare: complete original-English play

**Title, author and rights.** William Shakespeare (1564–1616). Composed c. 1605–1606, first printed in the 1608 First Quarto (Q1). A distinct edited text was printed in the 1623 First Folio; the two early texts differ substantially. We make no claim that a modern electronic transcription exactly matches either original printing. The original literary work is in the United States public domain.

**Text used.** The Project Gutenberg Shakespeare Team's `King Lear`, [eBook #1532](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1532), updated December 12, 2017, stored by GITenberg in `1532-0.txt`. Our entire literary selection is hosted locally as `public/books/king-lear-shakespeare-1532.txt` and is exactly the text between the main title `THE TRAGEDY OF KING LEAR` and the Gutenberg end-of-text boilerplate, apart from normalizing line endings. The source's Contents listing, Dramatis Personæ, headings, original prose and verse, character labels, dialogue, and stage directions are included.

**Completeness validation.** The 27,680 whitespace-delimited tokens cover all 5 acts and all 26 dramatic scenes: Act I (5), Act II (4), Act III (7), Act IV (7), and Act V (3). The final lines end with `[_Exeunt with a dead march._]`. The reader verifies section order and the ending, with 27 indexed sections (front matter plus 26 scenes). Paged reading does not abridge the text.

**Historic title page.** An authentic title page of Shakespeare’s 1608 First Quarto is available at [Wikimedia Commons: King lear title page.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:King_lear_title_page.jpg). The title page is public domain and used as historical cover art on the catalog and book reader. The title page illustrates the first printed edition, not the electronic edition's exact typography.

**Reader behavior.** `king-lear.html` uses the established narrated Shakespeare reader with Ophelia’s shared narration and sharing scripts: English voice via the reader’s device/browser (not pre-recorded audio), adjustable speed and size, page and scene selection, bookmarks, persisted reading position, and evening theme. The original work was written in English, so no additional translated/original-language companion is needed. Browser-device spoken playback, external cover loading and GitHub Pages deployment are separate QA steps and should not be represented as tested solely from GitHub source review.
