# Black Beauty — editions, completeness and cover provenance

## Book and public-domain status
- **Anna Sewell**, *Black Beauty: The Autobiography of a Horse* (first published in London by **Jarrold and Sons, 1877**), originally subtitled *His Grooms and Companions*.
- Original literary work is in the public domain in the United States.
- **Text source:** [Project Gutenberg ebook #271](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/271) — mirrored plain UTF-8 file [GITenberg/Black-Beauty_271/271-0.txt](https://github.com/GITenberg/Black-Beauty_271/blob/master/271-0.txt). Source transcription credits A. Light, Linda Bowser, and David Widger.
- The ebook's editorial notice identifies a **1911 American edition** as its transcription base, with minor verified corrections; this is not claimed to be a verbatim facsimile of the 1877 London printing.
- Local reading text: `public/books/black-beauty-sewell-1877.txt`. Gutenberg boilerplate and its editorial transcription notice were removed from the reading pages; that notice is preserved here. The author's dedication, contents, prose, part divisions, and chapter headings remain.
- **Completeness check:** 49 consecutively numbered chapters in four original parts: Part I (1–21), Part II (22–31), Part III (32–45), Part IV (46–49), ending *“in the orchard at Birtwick, standing with my old friends under the apple-trees.”* Local text contains approximately 59,900 words, including title matter and contents.
