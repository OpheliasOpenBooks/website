# Castle Rackrent — Maria Edgeworth (1800)

**Work and original edition.** *Castle Rackrent, an Hibernian Tale. Taken from Facts, and from the Manners of the Irish Squires, before the Year 1782* (1800). First printed anonymously for J. Johnson in London. The original 1800 title page has been verified independently and is public domain.

**Manuscript and provenance.** The locally hosted complete English text uses [Project Gutenberg eBook 1424](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1424), mirrored in [GITenberg's Castle Rackrent repository](https://github.com/GITenberg/Castle-Rackrent_1424/blob/master/1424-0.txt). Gutenberg's transcription is based on a Macmillan 1895 edition. We preserve the **original novel and its original paratext** from the author's 1800 Preface through the final glossary entry (source lines 933–4319): Thady Quirk's memoirs, the continuation on Sir Conolly, the editor's postscript, and all 29 glossary entries, including footnotes inserted into the narrative by the transcriber. To avoid misattributing later commentary to the 1800 novel, the separate 1895 `Introduction` and `Notes on Castle Rackrent` by Anne Thackeray Ritchie (source lines 63–932) are not included. They can be consulted in the source. The text is a transcription, not a first-edition facsimile; the PG introductory notice warns that some italics became capitals, British £ is represented as 'L', and notes and glossary references were reflowed.

**Hosting and checking.** Thirteen local JSON parts in `public/books/castle-rackrent-part-1.json` through `public/books/castle-rackrent-part-13.json`, totalling about 35,650 words, feed `castle-rackrent.html`. Word-order and complete content were checked against source lines 933–4319, ignoring whitespace only; all parts have metadata, sections and source-validated word-count thresholds. Part boundaries are editor-created loading units and do not add new book chapters. Original English is the source language, with no foreign-language companion panel.

**Historical cover.** Use the genuine [1800 first-edition title page, Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Castle_Rackrent_1800_Title_Page.png), which lists J. Johnson, London, 1800. It is marked public domain. The reader and homepage display Wikimedia Special:FilePath with a local neutral historical-cover fallback if unavailable.

**Audio and navigation.** Reader follows the shared Ophelia model: browser/device narrated audio, page bookmark and saved reading position with book-specific storage key, manual next/previous and auto-turn, text size and dark theme, section and part menu, shared text normalization and sharing controls. The 1800 Irish dialect is retained in displayed text. Live voice QA and GitHub Pages deployment must be checked separately.
