A note from behind the bookshelf

Help Keep the Stories Alive.

Great stories belong in the hands of readers. Ophelia’s Open Book brings classic literature together in one welcoming place, with books to read, voices to listen to, and illustrations to admire—without a subscription or library fee.

❦

A gift, never a requirement.

This little library is an independent project, created and maintained with personal time and expense. Every book added, every page prepared, and every improvement to the reading experience takes care and resources.

If Ophelia’s Open Book has brought you an hour of pleasure, an old favorite, or a new discovery, a voluntary contribution may one day help the collection grow. Whether or not you contribute, you are always welcome to read and listen.

I · Keep the doors open

Maintain the library

Support website expenses, maintenance, and the tools that keep books accessible online.

II · Enrich every visit

Improve the experience

Make reading, listening, navigation, and historically informed illustrations better for everyone.

III · A future possibility

Welcome more stories

Explore licensing copyrighted works for free reading and listening, only after the appropriate rights are secured.

A little gift can keep the stories alive.

If you would like to help, you can choose a one-time, monthly, or yearly contribution through PayPal. Gifts are always voluntary, and the library remains free to read and listen to.
