# Ophelia's Open Book — original-language translation audit

**Audit snapshot:** 18 November 2026 (main branch)  
**Scope:** Books identified in the website's catalog as English translations of non-English literary works.

## Reader standard

1. **English translations:** retain the current English read-and-listen interface, narrator options, reading progress, original book content and historical covers.
2. **Original-language works:** use a separate **reading-only** version with correct language metadata, chapter/volume navigation, font-size, night mode and saved bookmark. No original-language narration is enabled.
3. **Navigation:** provide a visible bidirectional link between the English reader and native-language text; include an original-language action on the catalog card.
4. **Text provenance:** maintain unabridged, public-domain versions in the United States where verified. Do not conflate translations with original-language editions; avoid edition substitutions and accidental claims of completeness.
5. **External archives:** distinguish external original-text archives or edition indexes from original texts actually imported into this repository. An external source link is **not** equivalent to a fully hosted original-language reader.

## Current coverage

- **41** English translated works have an original-language catalog entry.
- **16** English works have corresponding **hosted original-language reading-only pages**.
- **4** English works have **local original-language volume indexes**, pointing to outside scans/texts.
- **21** English works link to outside original-language sources or archives and still need local, complete editions if we want universal consistency.

The audit is therefore **not yet 100% complete under the strict “same exact format” requirement**, because a hosted local reader is still missing for all titles marked Index or External. The English narrators and original cover assets have not been replaced.

## Specific items requiring further verification

- **All Quiet on the Western Front** (`all-quiet-on-the-western-front.html`): original German *Im Westen nichts Neues* is **not yet linked** because a verified public-domain complete digital text suitable for reuse has not been selected. An academic publication-history page is not a substitute for a full-text source.
- **Ancient works** (e.g. *The Iliad*, *Oedipus Rex*, *The Aeneid*): ancient original language is linked to external manuscript/transcription collections; do not imply a one-to-one chapter translation across variants.
- **Folklore collections** (Grimm and Andersen): different English anthologies use different story selections, so a general original-language story archive does not demonstrate exact story-by-story parity.
- **The Song of Roland**: an Old French archive may show several editorial versions, so text equality with an English translation must be established before a local mirror is called “matching.”
- **Publication and geographic rights:** public-domain claims in this project apply to the legal scope of the source edition in the United States. Some originals and translations can be protected in other jurisdictions.
- **On-site rendered QA:** repository files and cross-link targets have been checked; a complete browser-based, page-by-page quality check, including speech timing and reader layout, has **not** been completed.

## Next editorial work

1. Import and structure original texts for the Index and External entries only after checking the corresponding edition and chapter completeness.
2. Check punctuation, prefaces, annotations, chapter divisions, exact language metadata, and end-of-book endings.
3. Keep original-language reading-only readers in the same antique style without adding voice features, modifying English narration, or changing approved English covers.
4. Repeat the catalog and sitemap link check after adding more books.

**Implementation:** `catalog.html` defines `originalByPage` and displays the correct reader/source on each mapped English catalog card. Original-language reader pages live at the repository root and are discoverable in `sitemap.xml`.
