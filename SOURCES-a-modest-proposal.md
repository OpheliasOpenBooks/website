# A Modest Proposal (1729) — Jonathan Swift

## Edition and source
- Original English-language satirical pamphlet anonymously published by Jonathan Swift in **1729**. Public domain in the United States.
- Text source: [Project Gutenberg #1080](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1080), [GITenberg plain-text transcription #1080](https://github.com/GITenberg/A-Modest-Proposal--13-For-preventing-the-children-of-poor-people-in-Ireland-from-being-a-burde__1080/blob/master/1080.txt). This is a later digital transcription, not a photographic facsimile of the first edition.
- Full title and subtitle preserved, plus every paragraph of the pamphlet, from **“It is a melancholy object…”** through **“my wife past child-bearing.”** 33 main-text paragraphs (3376 words; 3410 words counting the title information).
- This pamphlet has **no original numbered chapters**. The reader exposes only “Title and Subtitle” and “Full Text” for convenient navigation, without inventing spoken chapter titles. The Gutenberg text adds “by Dr. Jonathan Swift”, while the original pamphlet was anonymous; this is editorial identification, not a claim the first edition bore a byline.
- Only source header, ebook boilerplate, and hard-wrapped prose line breaks are removed. No text of Swift's essay omitted.
- An explicit description clarifies that the grotesque proposal is deliberately satirical, critiquing eighteenth-century Ireland's poverty, exploitation and public indifference.
