# The Death of Ivan Ilyich — Editorial Sources

**Author:** Leo Tolstoy (1828–1910). **Russian original:** Смерть Ивана Ильича (1886). **Public-domain English translator:** Constance Garnett (1915), original title usually printed as *The Death of Ivan Ilyitch*.

The complete 12-chapter English novella is hosted as a separate work from the Standard Ebooks Tolstoy short-fiction collection; other stories from the collection are not part of this edition. Typographic word-joiner characters and numeric footnote indicators were omitted to avoid disruptive narration.

- English translation and text: https://github.com/standardebooks/leo-tolstoy_short-fiction_various-translators/blob/master/src/epub/text/the-death-of-ivan-ilyitch.xhtml
- Original 1915 translation: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/79990
- First-publication Russian historical image (1886): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Death_of_Ivan_Ilyich.jpg
