# Moll Flanders (1722) — Daniel Defoe

## The public-domain text
- Full historical title: *The Fortunes and Misfortunes of the Famous Moll Flanders, &c.*, published anonymously in 1722; generally attributed to Daniel Defoe. Original English text, public domain in the United States.
- Text source: [Project Gutenberg #370](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/370) mirrored at [GITenberg, 370.txt](https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Fortunes-and-Misfortunes-of-the-Famous-Moll-Flanders_370/blob/master/370.txt). Project Gutenberg's transcription does not claim to be a diplomatic first-edition facsimile.
- Complete title statement, **The Author's Preface**, and the full first-person narrative, from “My true name is so well known…” through **“WRITTEN IN THE YEAR 1683”**. 136412 words in the narrative body, 138311 words total including title and original preface (word counts after source line-wrap cleanup).
- Original narrative has **no numbered chapters**. The 19 reading divisions are purely an Ophelia navigation convenience; they divide text only at preserved paragraph boundaries, do not pretend to be authored chapter headings, and are not spoken as part of the story.
- Only Project Gutenberg distribution headers/footers are removed. The hard line wrapping from the ASCII source is joined within original paragraph boundaries, excess typesetting spaces collapsed, and ASCII `#` currency markers followed by digits are restored to their intended pound sterling symbol `£` (e.g., `#100` to `£100`). All original story paragraphs and the concluding words retained.
