# Hard Times — editorial sources

**Author:** Charles Dickens  
**Title:** *Hard Times: For These Times*  
**First publication:** 1854, serialised in *Household Words*; book edition, Bradbury & Evans, 1854.  
**Text:** Project Gutenberg eBook #786, transcribed by David Price from the 1905 Chapman & Hall edition. The supplied Gutenberg HTML also features later-edition title-page information and footnotes; our reading edition contains only the full 37 chapters of *Hard Times*, arranged in Book the First (Sowing), Book the Second (Reaping), and Book the Third (Garnering). Printer's page-number spans, numeric note callouts, and the end-of-book editorial markers were removed for uninterrupted narration. The prose is otherwise preserved.
