How to Keep Your Books

A little bookcase, and no complicated rules

1. Collect a story

Open any book from our one shared library. Select “Keep on My Bookshelf” to mark it as currently reading. You can follow several books at once.

2. Follow the ribbons

A little ribbon marks every unfinished book. For supported readers, the last page you reached appears beside it. Select a cover to pick up where you left off.

3. Arrange and celebrate

Sort alphabetically, keep your newest additions first, or choose “My own arrangement” to move books left or right. When you finish a book, move it to your Finished Books shelf.

Finished books you've already saved here are preserved. Your shelves live in this browser, so another phone, browser, or a separately installed app may have its own collection. Reading lists are private and are not a second copy of the library.
