# The Turn of the Screw (1898) — Henry James

## Original work
- Henry James, *The Turn of the Screw* (1898), public domain in the United States.
- The story was first serialized in *Collier's Weekly* from January through April 1898, and first appeared in book form in the two-story collection *The Two Magics* (Macmillan in New York; William Heinemann in London, 1898). The second work in that collection, *Covering End*, is not part of this separate Ophelia reader.
- Text source: [Project Gutenberg ebook 209](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/209), GITenberg mirror [209-0.txt](https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Turn-of-the-Screw_209/blob/master/209-0.txt). The source file explicitly describes its textual base as the first American appearance of the story, not as a facsimile of a particular bound edition.
- Original story: 39442 chapter words; 42231 words total including prologue and small editorial title matter. The **entire original prologue and all 24 consecutive Roman-numbered chapters** preserved and 163 reading pages produced by Ophelia's existing pagination routine. Opening: “The story had held us, round the fire...” Final ending: “his little heart, dispossessed, had stopped.” Source line wraps joined within paragraphs, no narration/abridgment or extraneous Gutenberg distribution notices.
