# Tales of Mother Goose — source and editorial record

Original work: Charles Perrault, Histoires ou Contes du temps passé, avec des moralités, Paris, 1697.
English reading text: full 1922 Harrap collection, The Fairy Tales of Charles Perrault, translated by Robert Samber and J. E. Mansion, illustrated by Harry Clarke, with Thomas Bodkin’s introduction. 10 tales including all eight traditional Mother Goose prose tales and original English moral verses, plus The Ridiculous Wishes and Donkey-skin. Project Gutenberg #29021: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/29021 . Source mirrored at https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Fairy-Tales-of-Charles-Perrault_29021/blob/master/29021.txt . Historical 1922 edition is U.S. public domain.
Original French source read-only: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/20972 . This French later-edition transcription includes L'adroite Princesse, actually by Marie-Jeanne L’Héritier, not one of Perrault's canonical eight; French edition differs from the 1922 English edition.
Cover art: genuine yellow historical cover of separate 1901 Charles Welsh edition of Tales of Mother Goose, preserved at https://www.gutenberg.org/files/17208/17208-h/images/cover.jpg , source https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/17208 . Not a cover of the 1922 text edition. Both published before 1931.
Production: All eleven literary sections, including preface and complete tales with moral verse, locally hosted as eleven JSON parts.
