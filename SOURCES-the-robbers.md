# The Robbers / Die Räuber — Friedrich Schiller

**Original publication:** Schiller’s five-act drama *Die Räuber*, first published anonymously in German in 1781.

**English read-and-listen edition:** Complete public-domain English transcription from [Project Gutenberg #6782](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/6782) and its [GITenberg source text](https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Robbers_6782/blob/master/6782.txt). Source includes historical English translations of Schiller’s 1781 Preface, 1782 second-edition Preface, dramatis personae and all five acts. No unverified translator credit is asserted. Local hosting: `the-robbers.html`, 19 JSON reading parts (`public/books/the-robbers-part-1.json` onward), about 49,943 words. Narration uses the existing browser/device reader and speech controls.

**Original-language German read-only edition:** Complete German text from [Project Gutenberg #47804](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/47804), specifically the transcription of the **revised 1805 Cotta edition**; [GITenberg source](https://github.com/GITenberg/Die-Rauber-Ein-Schauspiel_47804/blob/master/47804-8.txt). Includes historical title/epigraph, persons, Vorrede and all five dramatic acts. The literary source spans lines 47–5810 in GITenberg’s text. The separate footnote appendix, publisher advertisements and later transcription/editorial notes are excluded; the source offers one explanatory printed footnote appended beyond the play. The German text maintains its eighteenth-/early nineteenth-century spelling rather than modernizing it. Text of approximately 42,602 words is stored in 18 local JSON reading parts (`public/books/die-raeuber-original-deutsch-part-1.json` onward), rendered by `die-raeuber-original-deutsch.html` with no audio or automatic translation. Gutenberg typography markers are stripped at display time, not from the stored source.

**Historically correct image:** Authentic illustration of the [1781 original first-edition German title page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Schiller_Die_R%C3%A4uber_1781.jpg), published in a Wikimedia Commons public-domain photograph. This artifact belongs to the **first 1781 printing**, whereas the German *text* is based on the **revised 1805 printing**. This distinction is clearly shown on both reader pages.

**Verification:** Both complete literary text ranges were compared to source material without loss, ignoring whitespace. Both five-act endings inspected, and manifests and reading-part metadata include language, part counts and word-count checks. The 19 English and 18 German subdivisions are navigational loading units, not original new acts. The two editions have distinct bookmarks, and both are linked in the homepage, catalog, and sitemap. The repository reader-format audit should classify the German page as intentionally read-only. Browser/device narration and live GitHub Pages loading require separate confirmation from static file verification.
