# The Beggar's Opera (1728) — John Gay

## Edition and dramatic completeness
- John Gay's *The Beggar's Opera* was first performed at Lincoln's Inn Fields, London, **29 January 1728**. Original English-language three-act ballad opera, public domain in the United States.
- Reader text: [Project Gutenberg #25063](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/25063), mirrored at [GITenberg #25063](https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Beggar-s-Opera--to-Which-is-Prefixed-the-Musick-to-Each-Song_25063/blob/master/25063.txt). This is the **1921 William Heinemann / Claud Lovat Fraser volume**, with the play text drawn from the **1765 edition** and scenes renumbered in a modern method. **It is not a facsimile of the 1728 first edition.**
- Includes **Dramatis Personae**, the **Introduction (Beggar and Player)**, three complete acts and **nine navigable scenes** as numbered in this edition, the Overture heading, all **68 AIR headings and song lyrics** (I through LXVIII), dialogue, stage directions, final reprise and “FINIS.” 17426 words of script and songs after removing the Gutenberg music/image placeholders and formatting underscores. Source poetry/stanza line breaks preserved.
- The initial 1921 prefatory tribute and editorial discussion are not part of Gay's opera and have not been included in the reader. The plain-text source has “\[Music\]” placeholders rather than readable musical notes: musical notation, MIDI and recorded performances are not bundled. Reader audio **speaks text and lyrics** via selected synthetic browser voices; it does **not** claim to sing the historical melodies.
- Original music of the ballad opera was arranged by **Johann Christoph Pepusch**; for bibliographic background on the 1728 printings see [The Morgan Library & Museum](https://www.themorgan.org/music-manuscripts-and-printed-music/111049) and [University of Virginia's edition overview](https://anthology.lib.virginia.edu/work/Gay/gay-beggars-opera).
