# Heidi — source text, edition rights, and historical cover

## Original work
- Johanna Spyri, *Heidis Lehr- und Wanderjahre* (**1880**) and *Heidi kann brauchen, was es gelernt hat* (**1881**), originally issued as two German books by Friedrich Andreas Perthes in Gotha, Germany. Both works are in the U.S. public domain.
- The combined story has **23 chapters**. Original volume I corresponds to chapters 1–14; original volume II to chapters 15–23. The local English text's chapter numbering runs 1–23 continuously, so the original volume divisions are recorded in the reader's navigation labels rather than inserted into or substituted for the translation's prose.

## Complete English text
- **Project Gutenberg #1448**, [Heidi](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1448), mirrored as [GITenberg/Heidi_1448/1448.txt](https://github.com/GITenberg/Heidi_1448/blob/master/1448.txt).
- English translator identified in Project Gutenberg catalog as **Marion Edwards** and elsewhere bibliographically as **Marian Edwardes** (translator's introduction signed “M.E.”). The original translation is public domain in the U.S. Attribution and spelling are provided in both forms pending full edition-specific title-page verification, rather than inventing a specific translation publication year.
- The transcription is complete and **not** the abbreviated/simplified 1916 Mabel Abbott edition. The entire text (approximately **91,720 whitespace-delimited words**) is stored as `public/books/heidi-spyri-edwardes.txt`.
- The local reading text preserves the original transcription's title and author, full table of contents, translator's introduction, and all 23 chapters, concluding with Grandmother asking Heidi to read her a hymn of thanksgiving. Project Gutenberg's older distribution boilerplate and footer have been excluded; attribution remains on the reader and here.
- Original German links, **read-only**, from Project Gutenberg: [volume I, *Heidis Lehr- und Wanderjahre* (#7500)](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/7500) and [volume II, *Heidi kann brauchen, was es gelernt hat* (#7512)](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/7512). No generated German speech is supplied.

## Authentic nineteenth-century cover
- [Wikimedia Commons: *Spyri Heidi Cover 1887.jpg*](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Spyri_Heidi_Cover_1887.jpg) is a photographed **1887 eighth-edition illustrated cover** of the original first Heidi book, `Heidis Lehr- und Wanderjahre`, published by Friedrich Andreas Perthes, Gotha. The image's artist is uncredited and it is public domain in the United States, marked public domain by Wikimedia Commons. It is a genuine period cover but **not** incorrectly represented as an 1880 first-edition binding or as the matching cover of the English translation.
- Cover is sourced through the Commons file endpoint, with a local neutral illustration fallback if Commons temporarily fails.
