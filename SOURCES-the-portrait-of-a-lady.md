# The Portrait of a Lady — edition, complete text and historical binding

## Original novel (1881) and later revision
- Henry James, *The Portrait of a Lady*, first serialized 1880–1881 in *The Atlantic Monthly* and *Macmillan’s Magazine*. First British book edition: London, Macmillan & Co., November **1881**, **three physical volumes**. First U.S. book edition: Houghton, Mifflin, November 1881 (title-page imprint 1882).
- James later substantially revised the novel for the **New York Edition (1908)** and supplied a retrospective preface. These are **different texts**; the 1908 preface was not part of the 1881 first edition.
- **This reader is a complete later authorial version**, NOT a facsimile of the 1881 first edition. The two Project Gutenberg electronic volumes include James's later preface, and the exact typographic/printing copy-text beyond that later revised-version status is not independently established.
- For readers comparing the actual 1881 novel, link to [Wikisource: The Portrait of a Lady, Macmillan 1881 original three-volume edition](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Portrait_of_a_Lady_(London:_Macmillan_%26_Co.,_1881)), with scans and transcriptions. Do not confuse the two electronic-volume Gutenberg division with the three physical volumes of the historical 1881 edition.
- Both the original 1881 novel and James's later authorial revision/preface are public domain in the United States.

## Binding
- Historical decorated cloth cover image: Wikimedia Commons [*The Portrait of a lady cover.jpg*](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Portrait_of_a_lady_cover.jpg), low-resolution 288×424 pixels, labeled there “Cover first edition” and public domain. Commons file metadata lists “1880,” which conflicts with the actual 1881 first book-publication date. Treat the image as a **period binding reproduced from Wikimedia Commons; specific binding issue/edition is not independently verified**. Do not inaccurately label it “the 1881 London first-edition binding.”
- A documented original 1881 London Macmillan three-volume first-edition binding is blue cloth with gold-lettered spines; see [Sotheby's, first London edition](https://www.sothebys.com/en/buy/auction/2023/fine-books-and-manuscripts/james-henry-the-portrait-of-a-lady-the-authors). The chosen Commons decorated cover could instead reflect an American binding.
