# Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas — source research and pending English edition

## Editorial status
- **Not yet in the public catalog.** Do not list this as an English read-and-listen title unless we obtain and verify a complete, public-domain English translation faithful to the original. The existing site rule requires full unabridged work and PD rights. Do not mislabel an abridged translation or a copyrighted modern translation as compliant.
- **Original:** Jules Verne, *Vingt mille lieues sous les mers*, serialized 20 March 1869–20 June 1870; original two volumes issued 1869 and 1870, illustrated Hetzel octavo edition 1871.
- **Original French transcription:** [Project Gutenberg #54873](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/54873), [GITenberg/Vingt-mille-lieues-sous-les-mers_54873/54873-0.txt](https://github.com/GITenberg/Vingt-mille-lieues-sous-les-mers_54873/blob/master/54873-0.txt). Locally mirrored as `public/books/vingt-mille-lieues-sous-les-mers-francais-1871.txt`, removing modern Gutenberg metadata and correction log but preserving title/imprint, both parts, all **47 chapters** (24 in first part, 23 in second) and table of contents. Around 881,926 characters and 139,464 whitespace-delimited words. Rights: published before 1931 and public domain in United States. Transcription explicitly states it reproduces the entire historical text and retains historical spelling. The text contains bracketed Gutenberg illustration references, which should eventually be complemented with period images rather than deleting prose.
- **French page prepared, not catalog-listed:** `vingt-mille-lieues-sous-les-mers-original-francais.html`. Read-only/no narration, section navigation, bookmarks, font size, and night mode. Once the English edition is appropriate, add a cross-link from English reader and discover it normally.
- **First-edition artwork:** Authentic 1871 Hetzel **frontispiece**, not exterior cloth cover, from [Houghton Library, Harvard University](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Houghton_FC8_V5946_869ve_-_Verne,_frontispiece.jpg), creator Édouard Riou. Reproduction public-domain in the United States and free for commercial reuse, with requested credit “FC8 V5946 869ve, Houghton Library, Harvard University.” Image URL: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/10/Houghton_FC8_V5946_869ve_-_Verne%2C_frontispiece.jpg.
- **Historical English options:** The public-domain **1872 Lewis Page Mercier translation** at [Project Gutenberg #164](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/164) omits material from Verne and is often labeled slightly abridged; scholars estimate over 20% missing. It is **not eligible** for Ophelia's unabridged-English rule. The **1876 Henry Frith translation**, a stronger historical rendition but still known to have some omissions, is digitized as [550-page PDF on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Twenty_Thousand_Verne_Frith_1876.pdf); a verified and proofread complete accessible transcription is not yet available. The full modern translation by **Frederick Paul Walter** at [Project Gutenberg #2488](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2488) is explicitly listed as **Copyrighted** in Gutenberg's catalog; public accessibility is not equivalent to PD rights, so it must not be copied into this site.
