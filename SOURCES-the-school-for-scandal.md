# The School for Scandal — Richard Brinsley Sheridan

## Work and public-domain edition
- Sheridan's **The School for Scandal** was first performed in **1777** at Drury Lane. It is an English-language original; no translation or separate original-language link is needed.
- The exact edition hosted in Ophelia's reader follows **Project Gutenberg eBook 1929**, an edited transcription prepared by Gary R. Young and presented from Fraser Rae's *Sheridan's Plays Now Printed as He Wrote Them*. It is an editorial manuscript-text version, with variants from some later stage/performance adaptations; the reader does not purport to reproduce an authorized lifetime first-edition text.
- Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1929 and the GITenberg mirror https://github.com/GITenberg/The-School-for-Scandal_1929 (blob 591d01c825f7cc1cb028eb903d04311f6d4e8140).
- Sheridan and historic edition text are in the U.S. public domain; no LibriVox or licensed recording is used.
- The source includes a textual/editorial introduction, *A Portrait* addressed to Mrs. Crewe, dramatis personae, David Garrick's prologue, **all five acts**, 13 original scene divisions, George Colman's epilogue, and five editorial footnotes. The PG transcription states that editor Fraser Rae's source uses Sheridan's manuscript and that some manuscript sheets are missing; readers should not mistake its editorial reconstruction for a single definitive author's edition.
