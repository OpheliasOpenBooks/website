# *Vanity Fair: A Novel without a Hero* — William Makepeace Thackeray (1847–1848)

## Copyright and original edition
- Author and illustrator: William Makepeace Thackeray (1811–1863).
- Originally serialized in 19 parts in 1847–1848. Published in one complete book edition in 1848.
- Original book: *Vanity Fair: A Novel without a Hero*, London: Bradbury and Evans, 1848; the original included engravings and woodcuts by Thackeray.
- The author's original 1848 writing and illustrations are in the **U.S. public domain**.
- Bibliography / rights: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/599 ; https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/357832

## Full unabridged text hosted on the website
- Source: Project Gutenberg eBook **599** (English), https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/599 (listed as public domain in the U.S.).
- Public-domain transcription mirror used for the local text: https://github.com/GITenberg/Vanity-Fair_599/blob/master/599.txt
- Local file: `public/books/vanity-fair-william-makepeace-thackeray-1848.txt`.
- Text has roughly **302,363 words**; it includes the original narrative introduction **“Before the Curtain”** (dated London, June 28, 1848), the contents and **all 67 complete chapters I–LXVII**, including the final “Vanitas Vanitatum” paragraph.
- Later Project Gutenberg volunteer, copyright-license and administrative headers and footers were removed, but the novel's original prose and subtitles were not abridged. Text has been paragraph-unwrapped for display only.
- This is a complete public-domain transcription, **not a diplomatic reproduction of a particular first-issue print copy**.
