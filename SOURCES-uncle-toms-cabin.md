# Uncle Tom's Cabin — 1852 novel, full text, cover, and narration

- **Work:** Harriet Beecher Stowe, *Uncle Tom's Cabin; or, Life Among the Lowly*. First serialized in *The National Era*, 1851–1852, and published in two volumes by John P. Jewett & Company, Boston, 1852.
- **Copyright:** The 1852 American original text is in the United States public domain. [Project Gutenberg #203](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/203) provides a freely available public-domain transcription.
- **Source text:** UTF-8 [GITenberg/Uncle-Tom-s-Cabin_203: 203-0.txt](https://github.com/GITenberg/Uncle-Tom-s-Cabin_203/blob/master/203-0.txt) (transcribing Gutenberg #203, with source header showing February 21, 2018 update). The text is mirrored to `public/books/uncle-toms-cabin-stowe-1852.txt` after removing only Gutenberg packaging before the book title and the distribution trailer beginning “End of Project Gutenberg’s Uncle Tom’s Cabin.” The entire novel is retained, including all original chapter headings, paragraphs, dialogue, italic markup, inline notes, and the two volume labels.
- **Structure checked:** 45 chapter headings sequentially numbered I–XLV. Volume I begins before chapter I and volume II begins before chapter XIX. Chapter XLV is **“Concluding Remarks”**. Ending of original transcription concludes with “wrath of Almighty God!” Locally mirrored text totals 180,616 whitespace-delimited words. It is presented as 46 navigable reader sections (title/volume-I front matter plus 45 chapters). The Volume II marker is moved from the end of the chapter XVIII parser slice to the beginning of chapter XIX for natural page layout; the source file itself retains the original placement.
- **Historic cover:** Actual 1852 original brown cloth cover of Volume I (not a modern recreation), digitized from the original [Wikimedia Commons 1852 Volume I cover](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Uncle_Tom%27s_Cabin,_-_Cover.jpg); public domain.
