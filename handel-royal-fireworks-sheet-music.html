Complete historical orchestral score

Music for the Royal Fireworks · 20 pages

This complete 1788 orchestral edition was published in London under the direction of Samuel Arnold, from Handel's composition of 1749. The first page begins with the Overture; the final page ends with the final Minuet and the printed word FINIS. The score preserves the brass, timpani, oboes/strings and bass parts, and is in the public domain.
