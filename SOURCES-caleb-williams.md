# Caleb Williams — William Godwin (1794)

Original first-edition title: **Things as They Are; or, The Adventures of Caleb Williams**. First published London by B. Crosby, 1794 in three volumes, with 12, 14, and 15 numbered chapters respectively (41 chapters total).

**Hosted text and edition:** Public-domain Project Gutenberg [eBook 11323](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11323), sourced as UTF-8 from GITenberg [repository](https://github.com/GITenberg/Caleb-Williams--Or-Things-as-They-Are_11323), file `11323-8.txt` (Gutenberg notes this transcription follows **London, 1903**). The 1903 historical apparatus contains a dramatis personae, an introduction by Ernest A. Baker, Godwin's original preface and a *related 1805 author's preface* appearing in the source edition (this later preface discusses a separate novel and is clearly labeled in our reader); all are retained. The original three-volume narrative is retained uncut, including all 41 chapters, with the concluding postscript. Original-English work: no translation or original-language companion panel. No Project Gutenberg license boilerplate is included inside the novel text; only the book and historical edition material.

**Reader structure:** `caleb-williams.html`, with 22 locally hosted JSON files: `public/books/caleb-williams-part-1.json`–`public/books/caleb-williams-part-22.json`. Part 1 contains historical front matter, parts 2–21 pair successive chapters within their original volumes, part 22 includes volume 3 chapter 15 and the postscript. Part divisions are editorial loading units, not original chapters. Source line wrapping is reflowed and some italic typographical markers flattened, while paragraph boundaries, authorial vocabulary, original chapter structure, and historical introductory material are retained. The reader has English browser-voice narration using `ophelia-narration.js`, familiar bookmarks and saving, per-part navigation, sharing via `ophelia-share.js`.
