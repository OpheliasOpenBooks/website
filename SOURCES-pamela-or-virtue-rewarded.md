# Pamela; or, Virtue Rewarded — Samuel Richardson

Original English-language two-volume novel published anonymously **6 November 1740**, first-edition printed title pages dated **1741**. This is the complete original novel, NOT the later sequel *Pamela in Her Exalted Condition*.

## Text
Text sourced from [Project Gutenberg #6124](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/6124), UTF-8 version at [GITenberg](https://github.com/GITenberg/Pamela-or-Virtue-Rewarded_6124/blob/master/6124-0.txt), blob 2a0aadb74fc4f4913567e5a2f800dea4284c234b. It is a complete public-domain transcription of the original narrative; **the particular historical print edition is not identified**, so it is not described as a diplomatic transcript of the 1740 first edition.

221400 words from source through final "THE END" including later publisher's note. All **32 consecutively numbered letters**, all subsequent diary/journal entries and correspondence, the moral/editorial conclusion, and the final words are present. Newline wraps in source were normalized within original paragraph blocks. Original headings retained. The source's much longer letter and journal sections are grouped for readability into **71 navigation segments**, without inventing spoken authorial chapter headings.

A **PUBLISHERS' NOTE** from the later print edition is included before the original story and not mistaken for an 1740 original preface. The original first volume and second volume are both present in the reading text; the source lacks explicit original volume break markers, and none have been fabricated.
