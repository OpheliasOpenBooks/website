Hallelujah Chorus · Messiah

George Frideric Handel (1685–1759) · Messiah, HWV 56 · Part II, concluding chorus · Composed 1741; premiered in Dublin in 1742.

Everything here is free. Listen to the complete Hallelujah chorus with singers and orchestral accompaniment, and follow its full conductor score. This is the complete chorus, not the full multi-hour Messiah oratorio. The illustrated cover is an original, historically inspired scene, not a contemporary portrait of the performers.

Free · Complete chorus conductor score

Hallelujah · Full SATB and orchestra · 20 pages

A complete public-domain modern engraving of Handel's chorus, with soprano, alto, tenor, bass and the accompanying orchestra: strings, oboes, bassoons, trumpets and timpani. This is the complete Hallelujah chorus score, rather than only a vocal extract or keyboard arrangement. Edited and typeset by Joshua Koo for the Mutopia Project (2015).
