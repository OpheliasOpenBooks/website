# Wallenstein — Friedrich Schiller: Complete Three-Part Dramatic Trilogy

**Work and order.** Schiller's Wallenstein, his trilogy of historical dramas about the Thirty Years' War, is presented in the original play sequence: I. Wallenstein's Camp / Wallensteins Lager; II. The Piccolomini / Die Piccolomini; III. The Death of Wallenstein / Wallensteins Tod. The works were written/staged at the turn of the nineteenth century; the public-domain first-edition title-page photograph shown on the site is from a **J. G. Cotta 1800 Tübingen** printing, not a claim that the hosted transcription exactly reproduces the first edition.

**English narrated edition.** 24 locally hosted JSON reading parts (`public/books/wallenstein-part-1.json` through `...-24.json`) and `wallenstein.html`. Complete public-domain source transcriptions from:
- [Project Gutenberg #6785, Wallenstein's Camp](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/6785), English translation by **James Churchill**, [GITenberg source](https://github.com/GITenberg/Wallenstein-s-Camp--A-Play_6785/blob/master/6785.txt), literary text source lines 30–1914; 3 reading parts.
- [Project Gutenberg #6786, The Piccolomini](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/6786), English translation by **Samuel Taylor Coleridge**, [GITenberg source](https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Piccolomini--A-Play_6786/blob/master/6786.txt), literary text and Coleridge preface/endnotes source lines 30–5222; 9 reading parts.
- [Project Gutenberg #6787, The Death of Wallenstein](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/6787), English translation by **Samuel Taylor Coleridge**, [GITenberg source](https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Death-of-Wallenstein_6787/blob/master/6787.txt), literary text and translator notes source lines 30–8059; 12 reading parts.

The Camp is not Coleridge's translation: in his own preface he explains why he chose **not** to translate it. The Churchill version therefore completes the trilogy without misattributing translation. The English trilogy has approximately 74,079 words. Source text was preserved in original word order, punctuation, speech headings and dramatic verse line breaks, with only whitespace changes at the start/end of paragraphs and encoding of reading parts. English narration uses the website's shared browser/device speech system, with bookmarks, page navigation, settings and sound controls.

**German original-language edition, read-only.** 25 locally hosted JSON reading parts (`public/books/wallenstein-original-deutsch-part-1.json` through `...-25.json`), and `wallenstein-original-deutsch.html` with no audio. Public-domain original German source transcriptions:
- [Project Gutenberg #6518, Wallensteins Lager](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/6518), [GITenberg source](https://github.com/GITenberg/Wallensteins-Lager_6518/blob/master/6518-0.txt), source lines 236–2585; 4 parts. Includes the separate early 1807 note with an additional verse, but excludes later ebook editorial correction log.
- [Project Gutenberg #6525, Die Piccolomini](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/6525), [GITenberg source](https://github.com/GITenberg/Die-Piccolomini_6525/blob/master/6525-8.txt), source lines 48–4886; 9 parts.
- [Project Gutenberg #6549, Wallensteins Tod](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/6549), [GITenberg source](https://github.com/GITenberg/Wallensteins-Tod_6549/blob/master/6549-8.txt), source lines 48–7582; 12 parts.

The German trilogy has approximately 61,504 words. These texts are from separate public-domain scholarly/electronic editions. They should not be characterized as an exact photographic or diplomatic transcript of the pictured 1800 first edition. As with the English text, all quoted literary source ranges were compared to the hosted JSON content ignoring only whitespace, and complete endings were visually checked.

**Historical artwork.** Authentic [Schiller Wallenstein 1800.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Schiller_Wallenstein_1800.jpg), title page of the 1800 Tübingen Cotta edition photographed from an original copy. The artwork is licensed via Wikimedia Commons; the website refers to this as the historical title page, not an invented binding.

**Catalog and publishing.** One unified library card `wallenstein.html` provides the complete English trilogy, with original German companion accessible as “Deutsch · Read only” via `originalByPage` and direct reader links. The library sitemap lists both pages. English and German bookmarks use different localStorage keys. The repository's static reader format audit is configured to recognize the German reader as an intentional no-audio original-language page.

**Verification note.** The source completeness check establishes that all selected transcribed literary lines were imported and in the right order. Browser rendering and spoken narration require their own QA, and the Pages deployment needs a successful GitHub Actions run to confirm publication.
