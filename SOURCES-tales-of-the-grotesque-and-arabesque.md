# Tales of the Grotesque and Arabesque — edition and public-domain provenance

- **Author/work:** Edgar Allan Poe, *Tales of the Grotesque and Arabesque*, Philadelphia, Lea & Blanchard, first edition dated **1840** (issued near the end of 1839). **Two volumes and 25 tales** (Volume I: 14; Volume II: 11), public domain in the United States.
- **Edition distinction:** The website recreates the **original 1840 collection's story titles, membership, and order**, but **does not claim to host a facsimile or exact 1840 text**. The locally hosted *complete narratives* were reconstructed from the later public-domain **Raven Edition** (five-volume compilation) at Project Gutenberg: [#2147](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2147), [#2148](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2148), [#2149](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2149), [#2150](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2150), and [#2151](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2151). The later texts may include revised wording and renamed stories.
- **1840 table of contents and preface:** Cross-checked with the Edgar Allan Poe Society of Baltimore [Volume I](https://www.eapoe.org/works/editions/tgavoli.htm) and [Volume II](https://www.eapoe.org/works/editions/tgavolII.htm); Poe's [original 1840 preface](https://www.eapoe.org/works/misc/tgap.htm) remains accessible in full via an **external read-only link** in the reader. The original table of contents and original preface are not integrated as a strict typographic facsimile.
- **Original title correspondence (later Raven titles):** *The Signora Zenobia* = *How to Write a Blackwood Article*; *The Scythe of Time* = *A Predicament*; *Epimanes* = *Four Beasts in One*; *Siope* = *Silence — A Fable*; *Hans Phaall* = *The Unparalleled Adventures of One Hans Pfaall*; *Von Jung* = *Mystification*; *The Visionary* = *The Assignation*; *Shadow* = *Shadow — A Parable*. All tales are indexed by the 1840 titles.
- **Contents:** 25 individually navigable **complete tales** (not excerpts or summaries), grouped into Volume I (14) and Volume II (11), plus the later Raven Edition's full **Notes to Hans Pfaal** as a separately identified appendix. It is **not** asserted that the later notes are textually identical to the brief appendix of the first edition. The local source is `public/books/tales-of-the-grotesque-and-arabesque-25-tales.json`, approximately 102,000 tale words before the appendix, sourced from the five unabridged source volumes. Front matter includes an explicit edition note.
- **Authentic historical image:** <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:GrotesqueAndArabesque.jpg> — scan of the **1840 first-edition Volume I title page**, hosted on Wikimedia Commons and sourced from University of North Carolina's *Documenting the American South*. This is a title-page scan, **not the historical bound cover**; the original volumes were bound in purple muslin with paper spine labels (Poe Society of Baltimore bibliography). The image is intentionally displayed and described as a title page rather than misidentified as the publisher's binding.
- **Reader implementation:** `tales-of-the-grotesque-and-arabesque.html`; shares existing Ophelia narration via `public/js/ophelia-narration.js`, sharing via `public/js/ophelia-share.js`, read-aloud voice/speed controls, book-specific saved progress, bookmarks, selectable tales, pagination, auto-turn and night mode, with no floating Home button. No LibriVox or prerecorded audio.
- **Validation:** The ebook source extractor checked that exactly 25 nonempty story texts were retained, the 1840 Volume I / Volume II grouping is 14/11, and the later Hans Pfaal notes are included. The reader validates story order and completeness before enabling the controls. Live browser playback and image access from visitors' browsers require separate manual QA.
- **Reader-facing catalog summary** intentionally remains a short, inviting synopsis; edition distinctions belong in this note and the source footer, as per Ophelia's standard.
