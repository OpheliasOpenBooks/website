# Sources — *Villette* (1853), Charlotte Brontë

## Original edition and copyright

- **Work:** *Villette* by Charlotte Brontë (1816–1855), first published in London by **Smith, Elder & Co. in 1853** under the pen name **Currer Bell**.
- First issued in **three volumes**. Historical bibliographic edition: <https://openlibrary.org/books/OL33224506M/Villette>; original first-edition transcription and source scan: <https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Villette_(1st_edition)>.
- Public domain in the United States: <https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/9182>, Project Gutenberg eBook **9182**, which explicitly identifies this work as public domain in the USA; Brontë died in 1855.
- **Complete text source:** Standard Ebooks transcription, <https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/charlotte-bronte/villette>; source repository <https://github.com/standardebooks/charlotte-bronte_villette>. Its English prose and original French-language dialogue are retained rather than rewritten or abridged.
- **Independent reference:** Project Gutenberg eBook 9182, with 42 modern sequential chapter labels, <https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/9182/pg9182-images.html>.
- **Chapter XI heading editorial correction:** Standard Ebooks transcribed this chapter title as “The Potresse’s Cabinet” (missing letters). The first-edition contents page gives **“The Portresse's Cabinet”**. The local heading is restored to **“The Portresse’s Cabinet”** without changing the chapter's prose; source <https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Villette_(1st_edition)>.
- **Original printed chapter-numbering caution:** The 1853 first edition was originally published in three volumes, and its original contents displays some irregular chapter numbering (e.g. missing XV and XXIX). For accessible reader navigation, this online edition follows the transcription's 42 consecutive chapter sections; this should never be represented as an exact facsimile of the original first-edition numbering.

## Completeness and editorial notes

- Local reader files `public/books/villette-1853-01.json` to `public/books/villette-1853-08.json`: seven sequential sets of **6 chapters each**, plus 253 editorial annotations / French translations in part eight.
- The 44 reader navigation sections comprise **a front-matter presentation, 42 numbered chapter sections, and one notes-and-translations section**. The 253 editorial notes are not claimed to have been printed as such by Brontë in 1853.
- Every one of the **253 note references** in the novel maps to one of those 253 notes; references appear as small superscript numbers, opening a dismissible note dialog. The reader removes the implementation markers from speech and visible text.
- Source italics are retained as editable markdown-like emphasis markers until the reader restores their formatting; original accented French phrases are left intact and supplemented by the editorial translations instead of translating/replacing source prose.
- Source text checked against the original final paragraph ending: **“Madame Beck prospered all the days of her life; so did Père Silas; Madame Walravens fulfilled her ninetieth year before she died. Farewell.”**
- Some source chapter spellings/typesetting differ from facsimiles. Where the novel has non-English dialogue, the browser/device voice's pronunciation may vary.
