# The Rape of the Lock — Alexander Pope

Original poem 1712 (two cantos); expanded five-canto edition 1714; revisions added 1717. U.S. public domain.

Text follows [Project Gutenberg #9800](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/9800), [GITenberg](https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Rape-of-the-Lock-and-Other-Poems_9800/blob/master/9800.txt), from Thomas Marc Parrott’s 1906 edition, ultimately using A. W. Ward's Globe Edition. **Not an original 1714 transcription**.

Reader includes Pope's dedication to Arabella Fermor, all 794 lines of five cantos (6156 poem words), Parrott’s detailed 1906 notes about this poem, and the original two-canto variant (1712) as a clearly labeled appendix. Other Pope poems in the anthology are not included. Original poetry line and stanza breaks preserved; editorial right-margin line numbers omitted.

Cover `public/art/the-rape-of-the-lock-1714-titlepage-inspired.svg` is a **new, historically inspired red-and-black title-page design** based on the 1714 first separate edition published by Bernard Lintott. This is not a facsimile. Reference: [University of Florida Special Collections](https://www.uflib.ufl.edu/spec/rarebook/catalog/pope/1index.htm).
