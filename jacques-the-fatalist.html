Jacques the Fatalist and His Master

Denis Diderot · Jacques le fataliste et son maître

On a journey with his master, the irrepressible Jacques begins to recount the story of his love life—but interruptions, diversions, and innumerable other tales continually pull the travelers from their course. With wit and mischief, Diderot asks whether our destinies are written in advance, or whether we are free to choose the roads we take.

An anonymous public-domain translation appeared in London in 1797 as James the Fatalist and His Master. An authenticated, complete machine-readable copy of all its volumes has not yet been prepared for our library. To avoid presenting an abridgment or a copyrighted modern translation as a full hosted audiobook, the English read-and-listen edition is not currently available.
