# Sources — *The War of the Worlds* (1898), H. G. Wells

## Original work and public-domain text
- H. G. Wells (1866–1946) originally serialized *The War of the Worlds* in 1897, and the first book editions were published by William Heinemann in London and Harper & Brothers in New York in **1898**.
- **U.S. public domain**: original work published before 1931. Text source: <https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/36>; preserved via <https://github.com/GITenberg/The-War-of-the-Worlds_36/blob/master/36-0.txt> (Project Gutenberg eBook 36). The electronic source is not claimed to reproduce the exact typography of the 1898 Heinemann first printing.
- The original **Kepler epigraph**, table of contents, Book One (*The Coming of the Martians*) with **17 chapters**, and Book Two (*The Earth Under the Martians*) with **10 chapters**, including *The Epilogue*, are preserved in the local reading data.
- Local transcription in four consecutive JSON files: `public/books/the-war-of-the-worlds-1898-01.json` through `public/books/the-war-of-the-worlds-1898-04.json`. About **59,755 whitespace-delimited words** including the two original book-divider labels.
- The original opening begins: “No one would have believed in the last years of the nineteenth century that this world was being watched keenly and closely by intelligences greater than man’s and yet as mortal as his own”.
- The original ending closes “And strangest of all is it to hold my wife’s hand again, and to think that I have counted her, and that she has counted me, among the dead.”
- **Transcription defect noted:** The Gutenberg source mistakenly prints “VI. THE DEATH OF THE CURATE” in the original Book Two table of contents and chapter heading, where it must be **IV**. Chapter **IV** in the reader's chapter navigation is normalized for correct book order; the actual narrative prose is untouched. The Gutenberg table-of-contents text is retained as copied to accurately document the source, including the typo.
