# Oliver Twist — text, provenance and reader validation

- **Work:** Charles Dickens, *Oliver Twist; or, The Parish Boy’s Progress*. Originally serialized in *Bentley's Miscellany*, 1837–1839; first three-volume book edition published by Richard Bentley in 1838. Original British-language novel, public domain in the United States.
- **Full English source:** [Project Gutenberg #730](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/730), transcription credited to Peggy Gaugy and Leigh Little, HTML preparation Al Haines. Mirror: [GITenberg Oliver-Twist_730](https://github.com/GITenberg/Oliver-Twist_730), file `730.txt`. Local site text: `public/books/oliver-twist-dickens-1838.txt`. Project Gutenberg legal header and license appendix were omitted; the title, subtitle, author, table of contents, and all fifty-three chapters were retained. **No chapter prose was cut or rewritten.** The original American-English ASCII Gutenberg typography and transcription are preserved, except that line wraps are joined for page presentation by the reader.
- **Completeness:** 53 chapters, consecutively numbered I through LIII; the opening begins with Oliver's birth in the workhouse, the final chapter `AND LAST` concludes with the marble tablet marked AGNES. Local text is approximately 897,635 characters and 157,981 whitespace-separated words including title/contents. This is a complete transcription, not a facsimile of the specific first-edition printing, and does not incorporate the first edition's engraved plates within the prose.
- **Historical visual:** Authentic *1838 first-edition frontispiece and title-page spread*, showing George Cruikshank's **Oliver asking for more** illustration opposite the Richard Bentley 1838 title page. [Wikimedia Commons original source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Olivertwist_front.jpg), a public-domain 1838 historical image based on a Heritage Auction Galleries scan. Used as the reader and catalog image, clearly distinguished from the original 1838 book binding/cover. The text and visual derive from different sources and are not represented as a single facsimile edition.
- **Reader integration:** `oliver-twist.html` reuses the Ophelia reader's original sentence- and punctuation-aware speech normalizer `public/js/ophelia-narration.js`, plus `public/js/ophelia-share.js`. Spoken chapter numbers use Arabic numerals. Includes independent saved reading position, bookmarks, chapter selection, text-size and voice settings, read-aloud, auto-turn, and evening mode. Narration uses the reader's browser/device voice, not prerecorded audio.
- **Catalog:** The home-page shelf entry in `index.html` is discovered automatically by `catalog.html`. Genre: Victorian Social Fiction. The site map is refreshed by the existing GitHub workflow.
- **Validation:** Source content checked for all 53 consecutively numbered chapters, valid start/end narrative, and sufficient text length before publishing. Reader code checks all 53 headings and complete final paragraph before enabling controls. Live visitor-device speech and image loading require an interactive-browser check; successful GitHub deployment, if recorded, does not by itself constitute live listening QA.
