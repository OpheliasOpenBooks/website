# *Arrowsmith* — Sinclair Lewis (1925)

## Publication and U.S. public-domain status

- **Book:** *Arrowsmith* by Sinclair Lewis (1885–1951), originally published by Harcourt, Brace and Company in **1925**.
- **Copyright in the U.S.:** public domain as of 2026 because the novel was published before January 1, 1931, exceeding the maximum 95-year publication-based U.S. term.
- **Bibliography:** The Morgan Library & Museum has a 1925 Harcourt, Brace trade edition of 448 printed pages: https://www.themorgan.org/printed-books/92978
- **Original full novel:** **40 chapters**, plus the opening title matter and Lewis's author's acknowledgment crediting Dr. Paul H. de Kruif for the novel's science.

## Complete edition actually published

- **Project Gutenberg eBook 70875**: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/70875 (released May 28, 2023; labeled "Public domain in the USA").
- Public Unicode text mirror: https://github.com/GITenberg/Arrowsmith_70875/blob/master/70875-0.txt
- Scanned edition identified in the transcription's imprint: George J. McLeod (Toronto), 1925, with copyright Harcourt, Brace and Company and first trade-edition printing note. This is a 1925 book-edition transcription, not a modern abridgment.
- Website preserves the **complete novel** in `public/books/arrowsmith-sinclair-lewis-1925.txt`, about **943,117 Unicode characters / 162,976 whitespace-delimited words**.
- Included: publisher title and imprint matter; original 1925 copyright/edition details; Lewis's full acknowledgment to de Kruif; chapters I–XL; ending explicitly marked **THE END**. Excluded: the Gutenberg boilerplate before the book and the later digitizer's errata notes after the authentic novel-ending marker. No sections of the book have been abridged or summarized.
- Rendering translates digitization underscores around italic text into HTML `<em>` but does not replace the original prose, and the narration strips those literal underscore markers only for speech.
