# The Three Musketeers — edition, illustrations and narration

- **Work:** Alexandre Dumas, *Les Trois Mousquetaires* (*The Three Musketeers*), first serialized in the French newspaper *Le Siècle* in 1844, and first published in book form in French in 1844. This is the first novel in the d’Artagnan cycle, with historical adventure, court intrigue, Athos, Porthos, Aramis and d’Artagnan.
