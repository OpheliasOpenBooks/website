# Manon Lescaut — Abbé Prévost (1731)

**Original work:** *Histoire du chevalier des Grieux et de Manon Lescaut*, first published in 1731 as the final volume of Prévost’s *Mémoires et aventures d’un homme de qualité*. Prévost wrote the work in French. The French text also received an authorially revised edition in 1753; the two cannot be assumed textually identical.
