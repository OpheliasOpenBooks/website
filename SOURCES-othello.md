# Othello — public-domain source and publication record

**Author and original publication.** William Shakespeare (1564–1616). Composed around 1603–1604, first printed in 1622 quarto; a differing version was printed in the 1623 First Folio. The English literary work is public domain in the United States.

**Hosted English source.** Project Gutenberg eBook #1531, *Othello, the Moor of Venice*, prepared by the Project Gutenberg Shakespeare Team, online publication 1998, last updated 2012. https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1531 . Hosted locally in `public/books/othello-shakespeare-1531.txt`. The literary transcription is retained from its original title through the concluding `[Exeunt.]` without abridging, paraphrasing, modernizing or omitting passages. Only line-ending normalization and omission of the external Gutenberg copyright/license wrapper were applied.

**Completeness check.** Five acts and fifteen complete scenes: Act I (3), Act II (3), Act III (4), Act IV (3), Act V (2). The entire dramatic cast list, settings, stage directions, verse and dialogue remain intact. The hosted text has 27,574 whitespace-delimited tokens. `othello.html` validates the title, cast list, act and scene counts, minimum length, the final scene, and the ending before enabling the voice reader. The reader features a configurable browser/device English voice, scene/page navigation, bookmarks, saved progress, and the shared antique sharing control.

**Historically authentic title page.** The image on the reader and catalog card is the first quarto's printed 1622 title page, a public-domain image from [Wikimedia Commons: O Q.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:O_Q.jpg). It depicts an authentic 1622 historic edition but the hosted transcription is *not* asserted to be a photographic or diplomatic reproduction of that particular quarto.

**Language and audio.** Shakespeare wrote this work in English: no English-translation/original-language companion is needed. No LibriVox or prerecorded voice is used.
