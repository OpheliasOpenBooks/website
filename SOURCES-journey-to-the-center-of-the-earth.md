# Journey to the Center of the Earth — historical edition and publication notes

## Original work and choice of translation
- **Original:** Jules Verne, *Voyage au centre de la Terre*, first published Paris: Pierre-Jules Hetzel, 1864, with **43 chapters**. Revised and enlarged in 1867 with **45 chapters**, incorporating two added chapters and restructuring the later part of the novel. Bibliothèque nationale de France edition record: https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb315627210.
- **English:** Frederick Amadeus Malleson (1819–1897), **1877** translation first published as *A Journey into the Interior of the Earth* by Ward, Lock & Co. It follows the 45-chapter enlarged novel, including Malleson's historically original translator's **PREFACE**, **CONTENTS** and chapter headings. Malleson's translation is substantially more faithful than the infamous 1871 anonymous Griffith & Farran version, which is an altered adaptation with names and passages rewritten. The translation is in the U.S. public domain; it is *not* asserted to be a line-by-line identical translation, and Malleson adds some headings and editorial interventions. **Do not replace it with PG #18857**.
