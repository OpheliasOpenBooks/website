# The Life of Samuel Johnson — James Boswell

First published London, 1791 (two volumes). Original English text, public domain in the United States. The complete annotated edition used here is George Birkbeck Hill’s **six-volume Oxford Clarendon Press edition of 1887**, NOT the 1791 facsimile and NOT the abridged Osgood volume.

Contents and sources: (I) Life 1709–1765, [Gutenberg 8918](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/8918); (II) Life 1765–1776, [9072](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/9072); (III) Life 1776–1780, [9180](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/9180); (IV) Life 1780–1784, [10357](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/10357); (V) Boswell’s Journal of a Tour to the Hebrides (1773) and Johnson’s Journey into North Wales (1774), [10451](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/10451); (VI) Hill’s Addenda, Index, Dicta Philosophi and historical bibliography, [11729](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11729). All six volumes come from public-domain Project Gutenberg transcriptions via GITenberg.

Original title material, Hill’s editorial introductions, Boswell’s advertisements and dedications, all prose, letters, travel journals, appendices, footnotes, bibliographical material and lengthy index are included. Gutenberg public-domain redistribution legal boilerplate is excluded. Printed hard-wrapped lines are normalized to paragraph strings where possible; indented verse and index material retain line breaks where feasible. Each volume is divided into **six editorial reading parts** at paragraph boundaries, 36 parts total, loaded separately for performance. These are *not* Boswell’s original chapter divisions. The exact historic printed typography is not reproduced.
