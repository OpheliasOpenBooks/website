# A Christmas Carol — historical edition, provenance, and reader QA

- **Original:** Charles Dickens, *A Christmas Carol. In Prose. Being a Ghost Story of Christmas*, Chapman & Hall, London, **19 December 1843**, illustrated by John Leech. Public domain in the United States.
- **Complete text source:** [Project Gutenberg ebook #46](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/46), GITenberg mirror [`GITenberg/A-Christmas-Carol_46`](https://github.com/GITenberg/A-Christmas-Carol_46), source file `46-0.txt` (UTF-8, editorial transcription prepared by Jose Menendez). Local file: `public/books/a-christmas-carol-dickens-1843.txt`. Gutenberg licensing and distribution boilerplate removed; the original title, subtitle, **Dickens’s complete December 1843 preface**, contents, and **all five staves** retained in their original sequence without condensation.
- **Completeness checked:** 5 staves, correctly named and numbered: I *Marley’s Ghost*; II *The First of the Three Spirits*; III *The Second of the Three Spirits*; IV *The Last of the Spirits*; V *The End of It*. Approx. 158,415 text characters and 28,541 whitespace-separated words including front matter; the opening sentence identifies Marley’s death and the final Tiny Tim blessing reads “God bless Us, Every One!”.
- **Authentic original first-edition binding:** <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Charles_Dickens-A_Christmas_Carol-Cloth-First_Edition_1843.jpg>. Wikimedia identifies this as the **1843 original publisher’s cinnamon vertically ribbed cloth** with gold title and wreath (photograph from Heritage Auctions, public-domain classification on Commons). Its image is used on the catalog and as the reader’s frontispiece. The first edition contained John Leech illustrations, but this site’s text-only transcription does not include their plates within the narrative; the cover is correctly identified as a historic cover, not a newly invented reproduction.
- **Other historical reference:** [Charles Dickens Museum’s original first edition](https://dickensmuseum.com/blogs/explore/first-edition-of-a-christmas-carol) documents the original cinnamon cloth with gilt details.
- **Reader implementation:** `a-christmas-carol.html` adapts the site’s established complete-text reader, with five individual stave selections, original preface as the opening section, independent saved progress, bookmarks, paginated navigation, voice and pacing controls, night mode, auto-turn, and sharing. Narration uses the site’s own browser/device speech system, not LibriVox or prerecorded files. No floating Home button.
- **Accessibility and quality checks:** Reader checks original title and author, five original stave labels in order, minimum text length and the final blessing before enabling controls. Static parser/pagination checks can be done without a browser; live speech playback, device-specific voices and remote cover loading should be checked in a browser following deployment.
