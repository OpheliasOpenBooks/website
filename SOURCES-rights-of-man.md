# Rights of Man — Thomas Paine (1791–1792)

Complete original English public-domain political treatise in two parts (1791–1792). The hosted edition follows the Moncure Daniel Conway scholarly edition (1894), transcribed by Standard Ebooks (2021, CC0 editorial contributions), originating in Project Gutenberg #3742. The hosted transcription is not a facsimile of the first editions.

Sources: https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/thomas-paine/the-rights-of-man ; https://github.com/standardebooks/thomas-paine_the-rights-of-man ; https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/3742 .

Contents: editor’s introduction; Paine’s two dedications and prefatory writings (including the English text of the preface to the French edition); the complete original Part I and Part II, including the **17 numbered articles** of the Declaration of the Rights of Man and of Citizens, all **five numbered chapters of Part II**, the 1792 French translator’s preface, appendix and **66 historical Conway endnotes**. Non-authorial Standard Ebooks imprint, colophon and license pages are excluded. Transcription markup is normalized to paragraphs: tables are preserved as text with separated columns, ordered lists are numbered, internal notes have displayed references, authorial text remains in source sequence, and long chapters are split only at paragraph boundaries. The thirteen reading parts are editorial for performance and navigation, not Paine’s chapters.

Files: `rights-of-man.html` and `public/books/rights-of-man-part-1.json` through `public/books/rights-of-man-part-13.json`. Existing shared English narration (`public/js/ophelia-narration.js`), device voice, paused position, auto-turn, bookmark/progress, share (`public/js/ophelia-share.js`), text size and night theme retained. Original language is English; no separate foreign-language panel.
