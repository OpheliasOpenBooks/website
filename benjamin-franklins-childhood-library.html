John Bunyan · 1678–1684

The Pilgrim’s Progress

Christian and Christiana journey through a world of trials and temptations. Franklin recalled this as an early favorite.

John Bunyan · 1853–1855 edition

The Works of John Bunyan

Three volumes of spiritual autobiography, allegories, sermons and religious writings. George Offor’s later edition is not the edition Franklin owned.

Daniel Defoe · 1697 · 1887 edition

An Essay upon Projects

Defoe proposes reforms to banking, roads, insurance, education and public welfare, including education for women. Franklin remembered the book as an influence.
