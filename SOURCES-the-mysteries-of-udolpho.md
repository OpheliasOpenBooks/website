# The Mysteries of Udolpho — Ann Radcliffe (1794)

Original-English Gothic romance, first published in four volumes by G. G. and J. Robinson, London, on 8 May 1794. This hosted edition follows [Standard Ebooks (2024)](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/ann-radcliffe/the-mysteries-of-udolpho), [source repository](https://github.com/standardebooks/ann-radcliffe_the-mysteries-of-udolpho), and checks against public-domain [Project Gutenberg #3268](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/3268). Standard Ebooks describes source text and artwork as U.S. public domain and its editorial contributions as CC0.

The text retains the opening epigraph, all **57 chapters** and their individual verse epigraphs, all authorial poems throughout the narrative, the complete conclusion and **four historical endnotes**. Chapter numbering in the hosted reading-room follows the Standard Ebooks sequence, while the first publication was a four-volume set; 19 groups of 3 chapters each are **editorial loading units**, not original volumes. Short prose and verse paragraphs are transcribed in source order, with hard line breaks retained for verse. Inline italic/emphasis markup is flattened to plain text; citations following verse are preserved as separate attribution lines where identifiable. Superscript note links are shown as [Note N] and are not spoken; the complete numbered notes appear after Chapter 57. Standard Ebooks licensing/imprint/colophon pages are not part of Radcliffe's novel and are not included. No LibriVox or other pre-recorded narration is used.

Reader uses the library's shared narration and sharing scripts, per-book bookmark and saved reading page, auto-turn, English device voices, settings and night mode. Original language is English so no separate read-only translation panel is needed.
