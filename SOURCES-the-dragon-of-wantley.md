# The Dragon of Wantley: His Tale — Owen Wister

**Edition and rights:** Original publication 1892. Complete story and original plates from the illustrated 1895 second edition, J. B. Lippincott Company, Philadelphia. Public domain in the U.S. according to Project Gutenberg; rights may differ outside the U.S.

**Sources:** Project Gutenberg #26448, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/26448 ; eBook transcription https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Dragon-of-Wantley--His-Tale_26448/blob/master/26448-8.txt ; illustrated source https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Dragon-of-Wantley--His-Tale_26448/blob/master/26448-h/26448-h.htm .

**Completeness:** Original dedication, original Preface, Preface to the Second Edition, chapters I–X, and original closing L'Envoi. 29,583 whitespace-delimited words in ten locally hosted JSON part files. Chapter headings preserve the original edition's old-fashioned u/v spelling. Narrative text not abridged or modernized. Standalone printer's illustrations labels, table of contents, image list, page reference notes, and Project Gutenberg electronic boilerplate excluded; original poetic stanzas preserve line breaks.

**Illustrations:** 12 unchanged image binaries from the original 1895 John Stewardson illustrated edition, copied into the site's own `public/art/the-dragon-of-wantley/`. Authentic title page, a chapter opening plate for each of ten chapters, and closing art for L'Envoi. No generated modern imagery used.

**Reader:** Full prose text, device narration via shared Ophelia narration module, automatic next-page reading, voice/speed controls, bookmarks, stored page position, text size, evening reading theme. Live speech and production deployment checks should be verified separately.
