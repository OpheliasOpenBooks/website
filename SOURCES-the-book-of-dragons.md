# The Book of Dragons — edition, public domain, and reader notes

- **Original:** E. Nesbit (Edith Nesbit), *The Book of Dragons*, eight dragon tales first collected in an early edition around 1901. Public domain in the United States. Period illustrations by H. R. Millar; decorations by H. Granville Fell.
- **Unabridged literary source:** Project Gutenberg eBook #23661 (https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/23661) from the archival illustrated transcription at https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Book-of-Dragons_23661/blob/master/23661.txt. The archive reflects a later Dover title page; no later introductions, additional stories, or copyright-protected modern cover have been reproduced. Eight original stories preserved in order with the author's dedication to Rosamund.
