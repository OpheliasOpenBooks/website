# A Sentimental Journey Through France and Italy — Laurence Sterne (1768)

## Edition, contents and rights
- English original by Laurence Sterne, published in two volumes in 1768. The work is intentionally left unfinished: its concluding words are “So that when I stretch’d out my hand I caught hold of the fille de chambre’s—”. Sterne never published a continuation or a visit to Italy. Do not add invented text or a translation/original-language link (English is its original language).
- Local content: `public/books/a-sentimental-journey-sterne-1892.json`, transcribed from Project Gutenberg #804, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/804, which states that its source is the 1892 George Bell and Son edition, edited by Henry Morley. Raw mirror https://github.com/GITenberg/A-Sentimental-Journey-Through-France-and-Italy_804/blob/master/804-0.txt .
- The imported text has 71 reader sections: opening, 69 episodic headings including the preface in its original place, plus seven historic footnotes in the final section. 39,186 text words excluding footnotes, and the final sentence validated.
