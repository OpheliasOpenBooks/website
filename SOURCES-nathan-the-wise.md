# Nathan the Wise / Nathan der Weise — source record
- 1779 original drama: Gotthold Ephraim Lessing (1729–1781), public domain in the USA.
- English translation: William Taylor (1765–1836), published 1830; 1893 Cassell & Company printing with Henry Morley’s introduction, transcribed by David Price. Project Gutenberg [#3820](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/3820), US public domain.
- German original: Projekt Gutenberg-DE transcription via Project Gutenberg [#9186](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/9186), produced by Mike Pullen and Delphine Lettau; 1779 work, later digital text edition.
- All five acts and the end of each play are hosted. English includes historical introduction, title page and dramatis personae; German includes title, motto, persons and five acts. English: 30921 whitespace tokens in 12 reading parts; German: 29278 in 12 parts. The local JSON, when concatenated and whitespace-normalized, was compared against the complete selected literary text from the Gutenberg files with no character differences beyond whitespace. No lines or acts have been condensed. Reading part boundaries are purely navigational.
- English reader is narrated with device TTS, with bookmarks and progress. Original German reader is separate and read-only, no audio. Each keeps separate progress storage.
- Historical artwork: photographic image of Lessing’s original 1779 book: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lessing_Nathan_der_Weise_1779.jpg), reuse permitted with attribution **Wikimedia: Foto H.-P. Haack**. The cover is a real 1779 historical specimen, but the hosted text is a later transcription, not a diplomatic facsimile.
