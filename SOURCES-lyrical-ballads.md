# Lyrical Ballads — William Wordsworth and Samuel Taylor Coleridge (1798)

**Edition chosen:** *Lyrical Ballads, with a Few Other Poems*, first edition (1798), London title-page issue printed for J. & A. Arch. The original publication was anonymous; the reader identifies both authors for modern browsing.

**Source:** Complete public-domain transcription, [Project Gutenberg 9622](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/9622), through [GITenberg's mirrored source text](https://github.com/GITenberg/Lyrical-Ballads-With-a-Few-Other-Poems--1798-_9622/blob/master/9622-8.txt). The reader includes the original title and imprint, 1798 Advertisement, Contents, all 23 poems in source order, original lineation and spelling (including "The Rime of the Ancyent Marinere"), the ending and historical poem notes. Gutenberg encoding/markup underscores for italics are stripped; editorial line indentation is lightly normalized for on-screen reading. This is a transcription, not an original facsimile. The later 1800/1802 editions, expanded poems and Wordsworth's later Preface are **not** substituted into the 1798 first edition.

**Hosted text:** Nine JSON reading parts at `public/books/lyrical-ballads-part-1.json` through `public/books/lyrical-ballads-part-9.json`, approximately 21,450 words of main text and front matter, rendered by `lyrical-ballads.html`. Part 1 contains the original introductory matter; the remaining parts collectively preserve all 23 poems. Splits are navigational reading units and may occur between stanzas without omitting lines. Text is English, the original language, so no foreign-language companion is called for.

**Cover:** Original first-edition 1798 title-page image, [Wikimedia Commons: File:Lyrical Ballads.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lyrical_Ballads.jpg); used as the opening image and catalog thumbnail with a local historical-cover fallback.

**Reader:** Uses the website's shared `public/js/ophelia-narration.js` and `public/js/ophelia-share.js`; read-aloud uses browser/device voices (not prerecorded sound). A distinct `localStorage` key prevents cross-book resume collisions. Includes parts, section selector, page bookmarks, highlighting, word-size / evening theme, and automatic continuation.

**Scope:** This is the complete collection as first printed in 1798, not a union of all revised later editions. The work and 1798 typesetting/title-page are public domain in the United States. Live browser narration and GitHub Pages deployment should be tested separately from repository checks.
