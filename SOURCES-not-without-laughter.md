## Edition and text

- **Original novel:** *Not Without Laughter* by Langston Hughes (1902–1967). First published in 1930 by Alfred A. Knopf, New York and London.
- **Text source:** Standard Ebooks, <https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/langston-hughes/not-without-laughter>, compiled from a Faded Page transcription and compared against historical scans.
- **Standard Ebooks source repository:** <https://github.com/standardebooks/langston-hughes_not-without-laughter>.

## Structural and editorial integrity

- The reader presents **all thirty original titled chapters in sequence**, plus **“To J. E. and Amy Spingarn”** dedication.
- Source parts: `public/books/not-without-laughter-1930-01.json` through `public/books/not-without-laughter-1930-05.json`, each consecutive chapter sequence.
- The novel contains traditional musical and hymnal passages; **71 source verse paragraphs** are retained with line breaks. The `[[VERSE]]` prefix is a nonprinting internal rendering marker, removed before speech narration.
- Original character speech, dialect, period terms, punctuation, and named references must not be silently rewritten, censored or modernized in the actual novel. The public catalog synopsis remains warmer and plot-focused and intentionally omits technical specifications.
- The reader validates 31 sections including front matter, requires substantial text, and checks the final line ending **“An we’ll understand it better by an’ by!”**.
- An added title/cover presentation is separate from the reproduced novel and is not a substitute for it.
