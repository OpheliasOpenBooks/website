# Sources and copyright — The Pilgrim’s Progress

## Work

- **Title:** The Pilgrim’s Progress from This World, to That Which Is to Come
- **Author:** John Bunyan (1628–1688)
- **Part One:** first published 1678 — Christian's pilgrimage.
- **Part Two:** first published 1684 — Christiana and her children's pilgrimage.
- **Copyright:** The underlying work is public domain in the United States.

## Complete literary source

Standard Ebooks: https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/john-bunyan/the-pilgrims-progress

Source repository: https://github.com/standardebooks/john-bunyan_the-pilgrims-progress

The Standard Ebooks text is based on George Offor's edition of 1855, with editorial normalization. The literary text and source contributions are released to the US public domain / CC0. The two narrative parts and Bunyan's original prefaces are preserved in the website without abridgment:

1. `public/books/pilgrims-progress-preface-1.xhtml` — The Author's Apology for His Book.
2. `public/books/pilgrims-progress-part-1.xhtml` — full First Part (Christian).
3. `public/books/pilgrims-progress-preface-2.xhtml` — The Author's Way of Sending Forth His Second Part of the Pilgrim.
4. `public/books/pilgrims-progress-part-2.xhtml` — full Second Part (Christiana).

The browser reader extracts paragraph and verse text from the four XHTML files. Links to later editorial endnotes are removed from speech/display so note numbers are not mistaken for Bunyan's narrative. These endnotes are ancillary 19th-century commentary and are not part of the original story. Original verse lines are retained.
