# The Pickwick Papers — sources, edition and validation

- **Work:** Charles Dickens, *The Posthumous Papers of the Pickwick Club*, also known as *The Pickwick Papers* (serialized 1836–1837; volume edition 1837). Public domain in the United States.
- **Unabridged English text:** Project Gutenberg eBook [#580](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/580), mirrored by [GITenberg](https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Pickwick-Papers_580), transcription by Jo Churcher and David Widger, revision dated 2016. Locally hosted as `public/books/the-pickwick-papers-dickens-1837.txt`. Gutenberg distribution header and license appendix were omitted from the reading text, while its book title, contents, detailed contents, and full novel were retained.
- **Completeness:** All **57 chapters** in correct order; original opening and closing narrative paragraphs preserved. About **300,084 words** including contents. The reader validates chapter count, ordered Roman numerals, text length and final sentence before enabling reading/listening. The text is **not** a facsimile of a particular 1837 printed edition; Gutenberg's transcription may synthesize printed editions.
- **Minimal correction to source:** Gutenberg #580's transcription mislabels chapter 44 as **LXIV** (64) and chapter 45 as **XLIV** (44), in both contents and main text. The local reading copy corrects the two chapter numbers to **XLIV** (44) and **XLV** (45), respectively, in both places. No prose was altered to effect these changes. This is a correction of chapter labels, not literary wording.
- **Historical cover:** A genuine **1836 illustrated wrapper from the original serial publication** by Chapman & Hall, [`Pickwickclub serial.jpg`](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pickwickclub_serial.jpg), historical scan hosted by Wikimedia Commons, marked public domain. This is not represented as the cover of a bound 1837 edition. The book's online reader and catalog reference Wikimedia's original image URL, rather than storing a new image copy in this repository.
- **Reader:** `the-pickwick-papers.html`, based on the existing `a-tale-of-two-cities.html` Ophelia reader, with `public/js/ophelia-narration.js` to normalize speech and `public/js/ophelia-share.js` to enable sharing; browser/device speech synthesis (no LibriVox or prerecorded audio). Includes independent saved position, bookmark, chapter navigation, auto-turn, voice/speed controls and evening mode. Floating home control omitted.
- **Catalog:** Direct home-page bookshelf entry in `index.html`, discovered by `catalog.html`; genre “Victorian Satire.” Sitemap includes new reader.
- **Validation status:** Verified source contains 57 ordered chapters and full closing paragraph. Static reader integration checked; live speech, historical image availability from individual visitor networks, and browser navigation still require hands-on acceptance testing.
