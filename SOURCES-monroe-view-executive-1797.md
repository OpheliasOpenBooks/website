# Source and publication gate — James Monroe (1797)

**Requested work:** *A View of the Conduct of the Executive, in the Foreign Affairs of the United States, connected with the Mission to the French Republic, during the years 1794, 5, & 6* (James Monroe; Philadelphia, Benjamin Franklin Bache, 1797).

**Publication status:** NOT YET PUBLISHED. Do not add the work to `catalog.html` or `sitemap.xml` until a complete, locally hosted, readable transcript exists and is verified against the historical edition.

## Incomplete transcription to avoid
Stanislaus Murray Hamilton, ed., *The Writings of James Monroe*, vol. III (1900), Appendix pp. 383–457, https://archive.org/details/writingsincludin03monruoft.
This volume reprints Monroe's introductory narrative **without the full body of instructions and correspondence** included in the original 1797 edition. Its digital OCR contains hyphenated line breaks, page headers and editorial footnotes, and must not be substituted for the complete 407-page publication.

## Mandatory checks before catalog release
1. Verify the **full 1797 publication**, including preliminary matter, narrative, instructions, diplomatic correspondence and authentic appended documents. Do not infer completeness from a title or a word count alone.
2. Verify the beginning and the final page against the original scan; confirm no pages or attachments have been dropped. Reconcile broken OCR, long-s typesetting, page-number and footnote artifacts without silently rewriting historical substance.
3. Host the entire English text on this website, rather than sending visitors to an outside reader.
4. Use the established reader from `hard-times.html` or `a-manual-of-parliamentary-practice-jefferson.html` including OpheliaNarration, Settings, narrator voice, speed, text size, evening mode, auto-turn, bookmarks and resume.
5. Catalog entry and sitemap must be created *only after* completion checks pass. Keep the book free.
