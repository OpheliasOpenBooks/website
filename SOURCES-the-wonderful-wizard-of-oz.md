# The Wonderful Wizard of Oz (1900) — L. Frank Baum

## Text and completeness
- Original English-language novel by L. Frank Baum, first published in 1900 by George M. Hill Company, Chicago and New York. United States public domain.
- Source: [Project Gutenberg ebook #55](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/55), mirrored in [GITenberg #55 plain text](https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Wonderful-Wizard-of-Oz_55/blob/master/55.txt).
- Locally stored as `public/books/the-wonderful-wizard-of-oz-baum-1900.json`. Contains Baum's original introduction, all 24 sequential chapters, and the final return to Aunt Em. No abridgment, movie adaptations, or Gutenberg boilerplate in the reader. The table of contents is represented by the reader's chapter selector.
- Original source's soft-wrapped lines were joined within paragraphs, and double spaces collapsed for reading presentation. Paragraph divisions and prose are retained. No text from modern annotated editions is included.
- 39299 source words including the introduction and locally added title attribution; the original chapter text and final paragraph were checked.
