# Petrarch — editorial sources and public-domain clearance

**Library page:** `petrarch-sonnets-triumphs.html`  
**Original-language page:** `petrarch-canzoniere-original-italiano.html`  
**Author:** Francesco Petrarca (1304–1374).  
**English book:** *The Sonnets, Triumphs, and Other Poems of Petrarch*, first Bohn's Illustrated Library (1859), reprinted by George Bell & Sons (1879). Historic translations into English verse by various hands; the edition also includes Thomas Campbell's `Life of Petrarch`. All original historic contents are retained.  
**English public-domain source:** Project Gutenberg eBook #17650, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/17650 . Machine-readable transcription: https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Sonnets-Triumphs-and-Other-Poems-of-Petrarch_17650 .  
**Original Italian work:** *Canzoniere* / *Rerum vulgarium fragmenta*, 366 poems, fourteenth century. The original Italian Petrarch verse is public domain. For a readable text, the local importer uses a digital transcription from Wikisource distributed by Libri di Dominio Pubblico (https://libridominiopubblico.it/libri/canzoniere/), with transcription attribution under CC BY-SA 4.0. It extracts and verifies **all 366 original lyric poems only**, with separate navigable poetry titles; the modern editorial introduction and source-edition metadata are excluded. The original text is not a modern English translation. Historic 1883 and 1918 Italian archive scans were examined as alternatives, but their OCR failed reliable source-integrity checks. The source URL, transcription attribution, verified poem count and SHA-256 digest are recorded in `public/books/petrarch/it/manifest.json`.  
**Illustration:** First historical English engraving from the Gutenberg 17650 illustrated edition, stored as `public/art/petrarch-1879-engraving.jpg`. Original Italian reader displays a historic page of the *Canzoniere* manuscript (British Library, via Wikimedia Commons, `Petrarch_canzoniere.jpg`).

**Copyright distinction:** *Petrarch's Lyric Poems: The Rime Sparse and Other Lyrics*, Robert M. Durling's 1976 translation and edition, **is not** public domain in the United States and is **not reproduced** here. This website instead presents the nineteenth-century English volume and Petrarch's original ancient Italian text.

**Reader conventions:** English text is locally hosted, read aloud with device speech, bookmarks, chapter or poem selection, sound and speed settings. Italian text is locally hosted, read-only, and has no narration. The English edition and Italian *Canzoniere* differ in editorial organization; a one-to-one aligned translation is not asserted.

**Source integrity:** `scripts/import-petrarch.mjs` checks both source identities, large lengths, known sections, and locally reconstructed texts; writes individually verified reading parts and a sha256 manifest, and adds the book to the home catalog only on successful import. Reading does not require a third-party website. Historical printer page markers and modern ebook production credits may be omitted from narration.
