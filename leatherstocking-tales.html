Original publication order: The Pioneers (1823), The Last of the Mohicans (1826), The Prairie (1827), The Pathfinder (1840), The Deerslayer (1841). These editions preserve Cooper’s original wording and include their historic introductions and chapter divisions. The narrative describes Indigenous peoples through a nineteenth-century perspective; some characterizations and language reflect the prejudices of that period.
