# Heart of Darkness — Joseph Conrad (1899/1902)

## Publication and U.S. public-domain status

- Original English work by Joseph Conrad (1857–1924), first serialized in *Blackwood’s Magazine* (February–April 1899) as *The Heart of Darkness*.
- First publication in book form: *Youth: A Narrative, and Two Other Stories*, William Blackwood and Sons, Edinburgh and London, **1902**, using the title *Heart of Darkness*.
- Public domain in the United States due to publication long before 1931. Project Gutenberg confirms US public-domain status: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/219
- **Do not substitute modern annotated or revised critical editions**, contemporary translations, or a shortened classroom adaptation. The local novel is a complete unabridged English text.
