# *Giants in the Earth* — 1927 complete English edition

## Edition and U.S. public-domain status

- **Novel:** *Giants in the Earth: A Saga of the Prairie* by Ole Edvart Rølvaag (1876–1931).
- **English text:** Lincoln Colcord (1883–1947), **in collaboration with the author**.
- **Original Norwegian publication:** *I de dage* (Book I, 1924) and *Riket grundlægges* (Book II, 1925).
- **First English edition:** Harper & Brothers, **1927**. The Project Gutenberg digitization used here is a 1929 A. L. Burt reprint by arrangement with Harper & Brothers, including the same 1927 English text.
- **U.S. legal status:** public domain. The original 1927 U.S. English-language publication has passed the maximum copyright term. Project Gutenberg lists this English book as public domain in the USA: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/75491
- **Original edition:** about 465 printed pages, in **two books containing ten chapters**, plus epigraph, dedication, foreword and introduction.
