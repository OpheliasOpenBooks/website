# Novum Organum — public-domain source and editorial record

Francis Bacon (1561–1626) first published Novum Organum in Latin in 1620 as Part II of Instauratio Magna. The work contains 130 aphorisms in Book I and 52 in Book II.

ENGLISH: Project Gutenberg eBook #45988, Novum Organum; or, True Suggestions for the Interpretation of Nature (https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45988). Public-domain English edition edited by Joseph Devey, P. F. Collier & Son (1902). Hosted in 16 local JSON reading parts totalling 91493 whitespace tokens. Includes Devey’s edition preface, Book I all 130 aphorisms, Book II all 52 aphorisms, numbered editorial footnotes and transcriber’s notes, with no literary omissions, save formatting normalization.

LATIN: Complete original Latin text hosted from the CLTK Latin Library public-domain corpus (https://github.com/cltk/lat_text_latin_library/tree/master/bacon). The original prefatory text (title page, prooemium, letter to King James, preface, division of the work and Preface II) and both full aphorism books are preserved in 14 JSON reading parts totalling 66872 whitespace tokens. A line break before Liber Primus XXV was restored without any change of Latin wording. The repository's source material is marked Public Domain Mark 1.0.

HISTORIC COVER: Engraved 1620 title page of Novum Organum / Instauratio Magna showing the Pillars of Hercules. Public domain. Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Novum_Organum.jpg . Artwork is from the original publication era; the digital transcriptions are later and are not facsimiles of this page.

READERS: novum-organum.html uses a self-hosted, English text with the shared Ophelia browser/device narration, chapter selection, bookmarks, voice and speed settings, and continuous reading. novum-organum-original-latin.html is read-only, without audio, and has separate page progress, bookmarks and text display controls. Each reader validates manifest-derived part counts and minimum word lengths.

QA: Source-to-hosted text was compared with whitespace-normalized parity, with all 130 + 52 aphorisms checked independently in both languages. Browser audio, art delivery and website deployment are subject to live verification.
