Ave Maria · Ellens dritter Gesang

Franz Schubert (1797–1828) · Ellen’s Third Song · D. 839 · Op. 52, No. 6 · Composed 1825. A luminous prayer beside a quiet lake.

Free listening and complete sheet music. Enjoy a full vocal performance with piano, along with the complete ten-page German-text piano-vocal score—not a simplified excerpt. The original illustrated cover was created for Ophelia’s Music Editions.

Original Romantic-inspired illustration of Ellen's lakeside prayer in the setting of Walter Scott's The Lady of the Lake. This is contemporary original artwork, not a historical facsimile.

Listen · Complete song

Performance: Dorothea Fayne, voice · Uwe Streibel, piano · 6 min 6 sec. Presented unaltered under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. Original audio and performer credits ↗. The recording is transposed from the original key; notation and performance may have different pitches.

About the words: Schubert wrote this song using Adam Storck’s German translation of a prayer from Walter Scott’s The Lady of the Lake. Its opening words are “Ave Maria,” but the original German lyrics are not the traditional Latin Hail Mary prayer. This edition follows Schubert’s original song, not a later Latin-text adaptation.
