# The Expedition of Humphry Clinker — Tobias Smollett (1771)

## Text and rights
- English original epistolary novel by Tobias George Smollett, first published 1771, public domain in the United States.
- Original source: Project Gutenberg #2160, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2160 ; GITenberg UTF-8 mirror https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Expedition-of-Humphry-Clinker_2160/blob/master/2160-0.txt . The eBook was initially released in April 2000, with update recorded September 2016.
- Full local text: `public/books/the-expedition-of-humphry-clinker-smollett-1771.json`. The original introduction features two prefatory letters: Jonathan Dustwich to Henry Davis and Henry Davis in reply, followed by the novel’s **83 letters** in original order. Each epistle has its original addressee as its heading, with dates, salutation, prose and signatures preserved. Roughly 147,837 words of letter bodies and preface, excluding headings. The source closes with FINIS.
- Original spelling, distinctive voice, dialect, epistolary addressee information and dialogue preserved. Only Gutenberg distribution boilerplate and hard line wraps removed. This is a public-domain transcription rather than a facsimile of a specific 1771 edition; paragraph and poetic line formatting may differ from physical editions.
