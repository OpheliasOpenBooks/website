Before you settle in

A few helpful details.

Is the book library really free?

Yes. You can read our Alice and Pride and Prejudice editions and use its read-aloud tools without an account, subscription, or library card. External sellers charge for their own products; a future Ophelia’s sheet-music shop would be separate from the free book library.

How does the narration work?

The reader uses speech voices provided by your browser or device. Press Read aloud to begin, then use Settings to choose a voice and speed. Voice availability and sound quality vary by device. The full text is available when sound is off.

Will there be more books and music?

That is the plan! Alice and Pride and Prejudice are available now. Dracula is next, with more books and a music collection planned.

What does “public domain” mean here?

We select older works whose copyright has expired in the United States. Copyright status can differ elsewhere. A newer recording, arrangement, illustration, or edition may have separate rights, even when the original work is in the public domain. Future music and shop editions will be reviewed individually before being offered.
