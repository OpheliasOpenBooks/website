# Bleak House — full public-domain text, 1852–1853 serial wrapper and audio reader

- **Title:** Charles Dickens, *Bleak House*. First serialized in twenty monthly numbers (March 1852–September 1853) and published in book form by Bradbury & Evans in London in 1853. Original English, not a translation.
- **Public-domain text:** [Project Gutenberg #1023](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1023), mirrored in [GITenberg/Bleak-House_1023/1023.txt](https://github.com/GITenberg/Bleak-House_1023/blob/master/1023.txt). Gutenberg's header credits transcription work to Donald Lainson, revised by Thomas Berger and Joseph E. Loewenstein, M.D. Header indicates an update on 21 February 2012. Copyright in the 1853 novel has expired in the United States.
- **Local text:** `public/books/bleak-house-dickens-1853.txt`. The distribution header before the title and trailing Gutenberg distribution/license block after the final text were removed. The original book title, author, table of contents, Dickens's **PREFACE**, all original chapter titles, and all **67** chapters are preserved in English. No abridgment, paraphrase, or modernization of the author's prose.
- **Integrity:** 353,462 whitespace-separated words including front matter. Verified chapters I through LXVII in continuous order, all 67 named chapter headings, the original preface, and the complete final chapter “The Close of Esther's Narrative.” The last chapter concludes intentionally mid-thought at “even supposing--.”; that is the novel's actual ending, not a truncation. Reader parses title/contents, preface, and 67 chapters as 69 sections. Historical punctuation and dialect remain intact in the published text.
- **Historical cover art:** Actual illustrated March 1852 **first monthly-part paper wrapper**, designed by Hablot Knight Browne (pen name “Phiz”). [Wikimedia Commons file: Cover, Bleak House (1852-3).png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cover,_Bleak_House_(1852-3).png). Public domain in the U.S. and its country of origin. The cover is an original serial wrapper **not a photograph of an 1853 hardcover binding**. Displaying this genuine 1852–1853 original artwork meets the historic-cover policy. Display URL: `https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/db/Cover%2C_Bleak_House_%281852-3%29.png/960px-Cover%2C_Bleak_House_%281852-3%29.png`.
- **Reader and narration:** `bleak-house.html` follows the shared Ophelia reader with English browser/device speech, narration normalizer, page navigation, preface, chapter selection, bookmark, retained reading progress, adjustable voices/rate/type size, evening mode, auto-turn and the shared antique share button. No LibriVox or prerecorded audio. Separate storage key: `ophelia-bleak-house-dickens-1853-v1`.
- **Discovery:** The home-page `index.html` book entry is automatically picked up by the independent `catalog.html`; categorized under Victorian Mystery & Fiction. The reader is listed in `sitemap.xml`.
- **Verification limitations:** Static source and code/reference checks confirm the addition in GitHub. The live Pages deploy, remote cover-loading in individual browsers, and actual narration quality are not asserted without device/browser testing.
