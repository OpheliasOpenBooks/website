- Full text: [Project Gutenberg #5230](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/5230) from [GITenberg mirror of the Project Gutenberg plain-text transcription](https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Invisible-Man--A-Grotesque-Romance_5230/blob/master/5230.txt), credited to Andrew Sly. Locally served at `public/books/the-invisible-man-wells-1897.json`; source paragraph content preserved with original soft line breaks joined. Gutenberg license and header omitted from reading surface.
- Contains the original contents, **28 chapters**, and the **epilogue** in 30 sections, 183 reader pages. Chapter body 47495 words; entire edition including title and epilogue 48322 words.
- Starts in Chapter I with “The stranger came early in February”, Chapter XXVIII ends with “his strange and terrible career.”, the epilogue ends with “until he dies.”
- **Important textual distinction:** The London 1897 first edition ends at Chapter 28. The 1897 American book edition includes an epilogue with Thomas Marvel. The Gutenberg source includes the epilogue, and is not claimed to be a literal transcription of the UK first printing.
