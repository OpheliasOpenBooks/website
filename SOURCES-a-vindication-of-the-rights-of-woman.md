# A Vindication of the Rights of Woman — Mary Wollstonecraft (1792)

Original English public-domain treatise, full title **A Vindication of the Rights of Woman: With Strictures on Political and Moral Subjects**. First published London, J. Johnson, 1792. The hosted text follows the public-domain [Standard Ebooks transcription](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/mary-wollstonecraft/a-vindication-of-the-rights-of-woman) (2024), based on [Project Gutenberg 3420](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/3420). Original English work—no translated edition or original-language companion is necessary. Standard Ebooks dedicates its editorial contributions to the public domain via CC0. This is a carefully prepared reading transcription, not a facsimile of first-edition typography.

**Contents:** Full dedication to Talleyrand-Périgord, author's introduction, all 13 original numbered chapters with chapter subtitles and internal Section headings, and all **37 numbered endnotes** as provided by the transcription. The 15 locally hosted reading parts divide longer chapters at paragraph boundaries solely for faster loading; they are not Wollstonecraft's original chapter structure. Direct endnote markers are retained as [Note N], with markers suppressed during narration and full numbered note text in the final part. Italic, emphatic and other inline markup is flattened into plain text; authorial paragraph order, historical spelling, punctuation, and embedded quoted passages are preserved. Standard Ebooks imprint, license and colophon pages are excluded, not the author's text.
