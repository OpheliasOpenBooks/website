# Ralph Waldo Emerson — Essays: First Series (1841) and Second Series (1844)

## Public-domain scope
Both books were first published in the United States in 1841 and 1844, respectively, and are in the U.S. public domain. They are presented **as two distinct complete, unabridged volumes**, not as excerpts from a later anthology and not as a newly condensed "selected essays" edition.

## Full texts
1. *Essays, First Series* (1841): **12 original essays** including History, Self-Reliance, Compensation, Spiritual Laws, Love, Friendship, Prudence, Heroism, The Over-Soul, Circles, Intellect and Art.
   - Local source: `public/books/essays-first-series-emerson-1841.txt` (412,588 characters; approximately 74,165 original-transcription words)
   - Reader: `essays-first-series.html`
   - Project Gutenberg eBook **2944**: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2944
   - Source mirror: https://github.com/GITenberg/Essays---First-Series_2944/blob/master/2944.txt
2. *Essays, Second Series* (1844): **9 original essays**: The Poet, Experience, Character, Manners, Gifts, Nature, Politics, Nominalist and Realist, and New England Reformers.
   - Local source: `public/books/essays-second-series-emerson-1844.txt` (327,747 characters; approximately 58,503 transcription words)
   - Reader: `essays-second-series.html`
   - Project Gutenberg eBook **2945**: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2945
   - Source mirror: https://github.com/GITenberg/Essays---Second-Series_2945/blob/master/2945.txt

The original book title, opening and inter-essay epigraphs, and complete essays are retained. The Gutenberg redistribution/license appendices and transcriber notices before the book title have been excluded. Chapter reading and pagination are generated from the preserved local source.

**Textual note:** the Gutenberg transcription of the eighth Second Series essay contains a typographical inconsistency, "NONIMALIST AND REALIST", in its roman-numeral heading, although its epigraph and the standard book title are "NOMINALIST AND REALIST". The menu uses the correct canonical title, while the original source transcription remains unedited.

## Historical illustrations
The original early essays were not illustrated in the modern picture-book sense. Both reader pages feature a public-domain historical portrait rather than falsely claiming an original 1841/1844 bookplate.
