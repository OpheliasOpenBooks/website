# Evelina; or, The History of a Young Lady's Entrance into the World — Frances Burney (1778)

## Rights, source, and textual edition
- First published **anonymously in 1778** by T. Lowndes in London, in **three volumes**. Written originally in **English** by Frances (Fanny) Burney (1752–1840); therefore no translation or additional original-language edition is needed.
- Public-domain base transcription: **Project Gutenberg eBook 6053** (early-2000s digital edition), https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/6053 , mirrored at https://github.com/GITenberg/Evelina-Or-the-History-of-a-Young-Lady-s-Entrance-into-the-World_6053/blob/master/6053.txt .
- The hosted English novel comes from this electronic transcription, **not** a diplomatic facsimile of a specific 1778 exemplar. The historical first-edition cover is separately identified. Burney’s prose and the period introductory writing are U.S. public domain.
- The Gutenberg file does not label original volume breaks in its continuous letter sequence. The website's three-volume split follows the **1778 first printing**, cross-checked with scan-backed original-volume contents: **Volume I, Letters 1–31; Volume II, Letters 32–61; Volume III, Letters 62–84**. See https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Evelina,_or:_a_Young_Lady's_Entrance_into_the_World/Volume_1 , /Volume_2 and /Volume_3.
- The original inscription to Dr. Burney, original dedication to the reviewers, and original preface appear in Volume I. The separately added Gutenberg digital table of contents and electronic boilerplate are not presented as Burney's literary text.

## Hosted reader and complete letters
- Reader URL: **evelina.html**. The text is hosted locally in three files:
  - public/books/evelina-burney-volume-1.json — original inscription, dedication and preface, plus Letters 1–31 (**34 sections**, approximately 49,582 counted whitespace words).
  - public/books/evelina-burney-volume-2.json — Letters 32–61 (**30 sections**, approximately 53,079 counted whitespace words).
  - public/books/evelina-burney-volume-3.json — Letters 62–84 (**23 sections**, approximately 51,384 counted whitespace words).
- Overall corpus: **84 consecutive letters plus the three introductory pieces**, approximately **154,045 whitespace-delimited words** (includes prefatory matter; approximate counting method is not an exact first-edition word count).
- Only one volume is loaded at a time, with a small cache for adjacent volumes, to keep the reader fast. Original paragraphs are retained, and hard-wrapped lines are rejoined; line breaks within indented verse are preserved. Letter headings are displayed and spoken as numbered letter names for accessibility.
- Reader retains the established Ophelia navigation, page/letter pickers, progress, shared English narration (public/js/ophelia-narration.js), speaker choices where supported by the browser, pauses, bookmarks, adjustable type size, evening mode, and the native share control (public/js/ophelia-share.js).
- The home bookshelf entry is in index.html and the searchable catalog is automatically generated by catalog.html, category **Eighteenth-Century Fiction & Satire**. Reader is included in sitemap.xml.

## Verification
- The local reader validates each volume's expected number of sections, letter numbering from 1 through 84 and the presence of original introductory material.
- Static verification should parse the script and simulate pagination against all three JSON files, ensuring every word is preserved and no page is oversized. Live audio/browser image behavior remains separate from static validation and should be checked after deployment, especially in Microsoft Edge.
