Arrival of the Queen of Sheba

George Frideric Handel (1685–1759) · Solomon, HWV 67 · Sinfonia opening Act III · Composed 1748, premiered 1749.

Everything is free. Hear the entire spirited orchestral Sinfonia featuring two oboes and strings, then read or download every page of its original-instrumentation score. Arrival of the Queen of Sheba · Thirteen pages

All 13 pages of the Sinfonia in the original orchestral scoring: two oboes, violins, viola and basso continuo. The complete final page ends with the closing double barline. This engraved edition was prepared by Vít Reichel for the Mutopia Project and dedicated to the public domain.
