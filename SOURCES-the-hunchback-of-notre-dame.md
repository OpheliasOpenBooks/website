# The Hunchback of Notre-Dame — editorial record

- Original work: Victor Hugo, *Notre-Dame de Paris* (1831), originally in French.
- English translation: Isabel Florence Hapgood, 1888, public domain in the United States.
- Full source: Project Gutenberg ebook #2610, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2610 (English; text archived locally at `public/books/hunchback-of-notre-dame-hapgood.txt`).
- French original: Project Gutenberg ebook #19657, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/19657 (linked externally, read-only; not narrated or automatically translated).
- This edition contains Hugo's preface and 11 books comprising 59 original chapters. The reader parser validates all book-by-book chapter numbers, requires substantial prose per chapter, and refuses a partial text. Source verification returned 59 headings in order and approximately 183,600 chapter words.
- Chapter distribution across the eleven books: 6, 7, 2, 6, 2, 5, 8, 6, 6, 7, 4. Long chapters are automatically split into reader pages without cutting or paraphrasing text.
- Cover: `public/art/hunchback-of-notre-dame-cover.svg`, a newly created antique-style architectural design; this is original artwork, not a reproduction of the first edition.
- The source has historical table-of-contents divisions and later notes included within the archived text. No rewriting or modernized adaptation is intended.
- The reader retains the shared narration script `public/js/ophelia-narration.js`, bookmarks, read-aloud, saved place, page navigation, settings, and Share functionality.
- Verification: text downloaded and saved by GitHub Actions; chapter distribution inspected against hosted source; live browser narration and deployment were not tested by this report.
