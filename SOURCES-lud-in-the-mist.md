# Lud-in-the-Mist — historical source and publication notes

**Author and first edition:** Hope Mirrlees, *Lud-in-the-Mist* (William Collins, Sons & Co., London, 1926). The original English-language novel is in the U.S. public domain. No later introductions or modern annotations have been reproduced.

**Text:** Project Gutenberg eBook #68061, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/68061 ; raw transcription: https://github.com/GITenberg/Lud-in-the-mist_68061/blob/master/68061-0.txt

**Completeness:** 32 original Roman-numbered chapters I–XXXII in their original sequence, 88014 whitespace-delimited narrative words. Starts with “The Free State of Dorimare was a very small country”; preserves the final “Columbine” verse. Eight locally hosted navigational reading parts (`public/books/lud-in-the-mist/manifest.json` and `part-001.json` through `part-008.json`), four chapters each. Hard line wraps in prose were joined, while original stanza lines are retained. Original work is not abridged. Gutenberg distribution headers and license are not presented as literary narrative.

**Authentic cover:** 1926 William Collins first-edition front cover, Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lud-in-the-Mist-1926-669x1024.png . Unmodified historical art displayed remotely with Ophelia’s existing local neutral fallback.

**Reader:** `lud-in-the-mist.html`, based on the existing narrated `a-princess-of-mars.html` reader, with shared `public/js/ophelia-narration.js` (browser device TTS), reading progress, resume, bookmarks, pause, auto-turn, text settings and evening theme. Original English; no translated-language panel required.

**Release note:** Browser-level speech, mobile and deployed image-loading checks remain separate from static validation.
