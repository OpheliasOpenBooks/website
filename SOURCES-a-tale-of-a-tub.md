# A Tale of a Tub (1704) — Jonathan Swift

## Original work and edition
- Jonathan Swift, *A Tale of a Tub*, first issued anonymously in 1704 by John Nutt in London; in the U.S. public domain. The original volume bundled companion works *The Battle of the Books* and *The Mechanical Operation of the Spirit*, which are **not part of this distinct reader**.
- **Source transcription:** [Project Gutenberg #4737](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/4737), via [GITenberg A-Tale-of-a-Tub_4737](https://github.com/GITenberg/A-Tale-of-a-Tub_4737/blob/master/4737-0.txt). Transcribed by Steven Rice, proofread by David Price from the **1889 George Routledge and Sons, Carisbrooke Library edition edited by Henry Morley**. This is not a diplomatic transcription or photographic facsimile of the 1704 printing.
- Locally hosted: `public/books/a-tale-of-a-tub-swift-1889.json`. Preserves **the original advertisement, the dedication to Lord Somers, bookseller’s notice, dedicatory epistle, preface, all eleven numbered sections, conclusion, the fragment `The History of Martin`, accompanying digression and continuation, closing project, and the 1889 edition's explanatory footnotes**.
- The 1889 edition includes material that was not in the original 1704 volume; it is identified here as appendices and notes rather than silently attributed to the first printing.
- 22 navigation sections in the reader, 41403 words in the tale through the conclusion and 45962 words total including the appendix and explanatory notes. All eleven sections checked in sequence. Source line wraps joined within paragraphs, type-setting underscores removed as plain-text italic markers. Page numbers of the 1889 edition are omitted from navigation, and Gutenberg notices/licensing boilerplate and placeholder image labels omitted from the reader. Footnote references remain visible in the prose but are suppressed from spoken narration.
