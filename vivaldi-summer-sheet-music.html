L’estate (Summer)

Antonio Vivaldi (1678–1741) · The Four Seasons · Concerto No. 2 in G minor · Op. 8, No. 2 · RV 315 · First published 1725.

Everything is free. Listen to all three complete concerto movements in order, and follow Vivaldi’s orchestra in a complete conductor score. This ornate cover is Ophelia’s original modern illustration inspired by the eighteenth century, rather than an authentic 1725 printing.
